A sentence is a group of words that expresses a complete idea. In English, words usually follow a specific order.

Understanding how to arrange words helps you create clear and correct sentences.

---

## Basic Sentence Structure

The most basic English sentence follows this pattern:

- #### Subject + Verb
  - I study.
  - She works.
  - They play.
  - Birds fly.
  - John sleeps.

The subject tells us who or what the sentence is about.

The verb tells us what the subject does or what happens.

---

## Subject + Verb + Object

Many English sentences use an object after the verb.

The basic pattern is: **_Subject + Verb + Object_**

- #### Examples
  - I read books.
  - She likes music.
  - Tom plays football.
  - We study English.
  - They eat breakfast.

The object receives the action of the verb.

### Example

Sarah reads a book.

- Subject: Sarah
- Verb: reads
- Object: a book

Sarah performs the action, and a book receives the action.

---

## Subject + Verb + Complement

Some sentences use a complement instead of an object.

The basic pattern is: **_Subject + Verb + Complement_**

- #### Examples
  - I am happy.
  - She is a teacher.
  - They are tired.
  - The food is delicious.
  - John is my brother.

The complement gives more information about the subject.

---

## Adding More Information

You can add additional information to a sentence, such as place, time, or manner.

- #### Place
  - I study English at home.
  - She works at the office.
- #### Time
  - They play football every Saturday.
  - I study English every day.
- #### Manner
  - She speaks slowly.
  - He runs quickly.

You can combine different types of information.

I study English at home every day.

---

## Building a Sentence Step by Step

You can construct a sentence by adding words in stages.

1. Choose a Subject (I)
2. Add a Verb (I study)
3. Add an Object (I study English)
4. Add More Information (I study English every day.)

The complete sentence is: _I study English every day._

### Another Example

The boy

- -> The boy plays
- -> The boy plays football
- -> The boy plays football in the park.

## Common Sentence Patterns

English uses several common sentence patterns.

| Pattern                         | Example                            |
| ------------------------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| Subject + Verb                  | Birds fly.                         |
| Subject + Verb + Object         | I read books.                      |
| Subject + Verb + Complement     | She is happy.                      |
| Subject + Verb + Place          | They live in Chile.                |
| Subject + Verb + Time           | We study every day.                |
| Subject + Verb + Object + Place | I read books at home.              |
| Subject + Verb + Object + Time  | She studies English every morning. |

The most important pattern for beginners is:

**_Subject + Verb + Object_**

## Word Order

English generally follows a fixed word order.

Subject → Verb → Object

- #### Correct
  - I drink water.
- #### Incorrect
  - I water drink.

The words are the same, but their order is incorrect.

### Another example:

- She reads books.
- Not:
  - She books reads.

Correct word order makes the meaning clear.

---

## Capital Letters

The first word of a sentence normally begins with a capital letter.

- #### Correct
  - My brother plays football.
- #### Incorrect
  - my brother plays football.

Names and proper nouns also begin with capital letters.

- John lives in Chile.
- Maria studies English.
- I live in Chile.

---

## End Punctuation

A sentence normally ends with punctuation.

### Period (.)

Used for statements.

- I study English.

### Question Mark (?)

Used for questions.

- Do you speak English?

### Exclamation Mark (!)

Used to express strong emotion or emphasis.

- That's great!

---

## 10. Common Mistakes

### Incorrect Word Order

- ❌ I English study.
- ✅ I study English.

### Missing Subject

- ❌ Study English every day.

For a basic statement, include the subject:

- ✅ I study English every day.

### Missing Verb

- ❌ She very happy.
- ✅ She is very happy.

### Missing Punctuation

- ❌ I like music
- ✅ I like music.
