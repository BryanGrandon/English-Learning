## What Are Prepositions of Time?

Prepositions of time tell us **when something happens**.

The three most important prepositions of time at A1 are:

- **at · on · in**

They are used with different types of time expressions.

---

## At

Use **at** with specific times.

- #### Examples:
  - The class starts **at 8:00**.
  - I get up **at 7 o'clock**.
  - We have lunch **at noon**.
  - She goes to bed **at night**.

### Common Expressions with At

- Clock times: at 7:00, at 10:30
- Specific points in time: at noon, at midnight
- Some fixed expressions: at night, at the weekend

---

## On

Use **on** with days and dates.

- #### Examples:
  - I work **on Monday**.
  - We have English class **on Tuesday**.
  - My birthday is **on June 15th**.
  - They arrive **on Friday morning**.

### Common Expressions with On

- Days: on Monday, on Friday
- Dates: on July 15th, on December 25th
- Specific days: on my birthday, on the weekend

---

## In

Use **in** with longer periods of time.

- #### Examples:
  - I was born **in 2005**.
  - We travel **in July**.
  - She studies **in the morning**.
  - They play football **in the afternoon**.
  - I usually read **in the evening**.

### Common Expressions with In

- Months: in January, in July
- Years: in 2026, in 1999
- Seasons: in summer, in winter
- Parts of the day: in the morning, in the afternoon, in the evening

---

## Comparison At, On, and In

The choice depends on the time expression.

| Preposition | Used with        | Example        |
| ----------- | ---------------- | -------------- |
| **at**      | exact times      | at 7:30        |
| **on**      | days             | on Monday      |
| **on**      | dates            | on May 10th    |
| **in**      | months           | in May         |
| **in**      | years            | in 2026        |
| **in**      | seasons          | in summer      |
| **in**      | parts of the day | in the morning |

Think of the general pattern:

- **at → exact point**
- **on → day/date**
- **in → longer period**

---

## Common Exceptions

Some expressions do not follow the basic pattern exactly.

We say:

- **at night**
- **in the morning**
- **in the afternoon**
- **in the evening**

Compare:

- I study **in the evening**.
- I sleep **at night**.

We also commonly say:

- **at the weekend** in British English
- **on the weekend** in American English

---

## Common Mistakes

### at (exact time) / in (longer period)

- ❌ I wake up in 7:00.
- ✅ I wake up at 7:00.

### in (longer period) / on (day / date)

- ❌ We have class in Monday.
- ✅ We have class on Monday.
- ❌ My birthday is on July.
- ✅ My birthday is in July.
- ❌ She was born on 2005.
- ✅ She was born in 2005.

---

## Quick Summary

Use:

- **AT** → exact times
- **ON** → days and dates
- **IN** → months, years, seasons, and longer periods

Examples:

- **at** 8:00
- **on** Monday
- **on** June 10th
- **in** June
- **in** 2026
- **in** summer
- **in** the morning
