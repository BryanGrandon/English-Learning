## What Are Prepositions of Place?

Prepositions of place describe **where a person or thing is located**.

Common prepositions of place include:

- **in · on · at · under · next to · behind · in front of · between · near**

### Examples

- The book is **on** the table.
- The cat is **under** the chair.
- The school is **near** my house.

---

## In

Use **in** when something is inside a space, area, or container.

Examples:

- The keys are **in** the bag.
- She is **in** the classroom.
- There is water **in** the bottle.
- They live **in** Chile.

Think:

- **in = inside**

---

## On

Use **on** when something is touching a surface.

Examples:

- The book is **on** the table.
- The phone is **on** the desk.
- There is a picture **on** the wall.
- The keys are **on** the floor.

Think:

- **on = touching a surface**

---

## At

Use **at** to talk about a specific point or location.

Examples:

- She is **at** school.
- He is **at** the bus stop.
- We are **at** the door.
- They are **at** the station.

Compare:

- She is **in** the school. -> inside the building.
- She is **at** school. -> at that location/institution.

---

## Under

**Under** means that something is below another thing.

Examples:

- The cat is **under** the table.
- The shoes are **under** the bed.
- The bag is **under** the chair.

---

## Next To

**Next to** means directly beside something or someone.

Examples:

- The bank is **next to** the supermarket.
- Sarah is sitting **next to** John.
- The chair is **next to** the table.

---

## Behind

**Behind** means at the back of something.

Examples:

- The car is **behind** the house.
- The garden is **behind** the building.
- The cat is **behind** the door.

---

## In Front Of

**In front of** means before or ahead of something.

Examples:

- The car is **in front of** the house.
- The teacher is **in front of** the students.
- There is a bus **in front of** the school.

Compare:

- The car is **behind** the house.
- The car is **in front of** the house.

---

## Between

**Between** means in the space separating two people or things.

Examples:

- The school is **between** the bank and the library.
- The cat is **between** the chairs.
- John is sitting **between** Anna and Mark.

---

## Near

**Near** means close to something or someone.

Examples:

- The supermarket is **near** my house.
- There is a park **near** the school.
- She lives **near** the station.

---

## Common Prepositions of Place

| Preposition     | Basic meaning         | Example                                    |
| --------------- | --------------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| **in**          | inside                | The keys are in the bag.                   |
| **on**          | on a surface          | The book is on the desk.                   |
| **at**          | specific location     | She is at school.                          |
| **under**       | below                 | The cat is under the table.                |
| **next to**     | beside                | The bank is next to the hotel.             |
| **behind**      | at the back           | The car is behind the house.               |
| **in front of** | ahead of              | The bus is in front of the school.         |
| **between**     | separating two things | The shop is between the bank and the café. |
| **near**        | close to              | The park is near my house.                 |

---

## Common Mistakes

### In vs. On

- ❌ The book is in the table.
- ✅ The book is on the table.

### Behind vs. In Front Of

- ❌ The teacher is behind the students.
  - -> This means the teacher is at their back.
- ✅ The teacher is in front of the students.

### Between

Use **between** when something is located in the space separating two things.

- The café is **between the bank and the hotel**.

---

## Quick Summary

Prepositions of place tell us **where something or someone is**.

Remember the most common patterns:

- **in** → inside
- **on** → on a surface
- **at** → a specific location
- **under** → below
- **next to** → beside
- **behind** → at the back
- **in front of** → ahead
- **between** → separating two things
- **near** → close
