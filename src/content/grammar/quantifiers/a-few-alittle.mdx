**A few** and **a little** are used to talk about a small quantity or number.

The choice depends on the type of noun.

---

## A Few

Use **a few** with **plural countable nouns**.

- I have **a few books**.
- She has **a few friends**.
- There are **a few students** in the classroom.
- We need **a few apples**.

> **a few + plural countable noun**

**A few** means a small number, but enough for the situation.

- I have **a few minutes**.
  → I have some minutes available.

---

## A Little

Use **a little** with **uncountable nouns**.

- I have **a little money**.
- There is **a little water** left.
- She has **a little time**.
- We need **a little sugar**.

> **a little + uncountable noun**

**A little** means a small amount, but some is available.

---

## A Few vs. A Little

| Quantifier   | Noun type        | Example        |
| ------------ | ---------------- | -------------- |
| **a few**    | Plural countable | a few books    |
| **a little** | Uncountable      | a little water |

- #### Compare:
  - I have **a few books**.
  - I have **a little money**.

---

## A Few and A Little in Sentences

### A Few

- I have **a few questions**.
- We need **a few chairs**.
- There are **a few restaurants** nearby.

### A Little

- I need **a little help**.
- There is **a little milk** in the fridge.
- We have **a little time**.

---

## A Few vs. A Little Without "A"

Be careful with **few** and **little**.

At A1 level, focus mainly on:

> **a few → some small number**
> **a little → some small amount**

Compare:

- I have **a few friends**. → I have some friends.
- I have **a little money**. → I have some money.

The expressions **few** and **little** without **a** have a different meaning and can be studied at a higher level.

---

## Common Mistakes

- ❌ a little **books**
- ✅ a few **books**
- ❌ a few **water**
- ✅ a little **water**
- ❌ a little **apples**
- ✅ a few **apples**
- ❌ a few **money**
- ✅ a little **money**

---

## Quick Summary

- **A few** is used with plural countable nouns.
- **A little** is used with uncountable nouns.
- **A few** → a few books, a few people.
- **A little** → a little water, a little time.
- Both describe a small quantity or number.
