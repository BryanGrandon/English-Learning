**Some** and **any** are used to talk about an indefinite quantity or number.

They can be used with **plural countable nouns** and **uncountable nouns**.

---

## Some

We usually use **some** in affirmative sentences.

### With plural countable nouns

- I have **some books**.
- There are **some apples** on the table.
- She has **some friends** in London.

### With uncountable nouns

- I need **some water**.
- There is **some milk** in the fridge.
- We have **some information**.

### Structure

- > **some + plural countable noun**
- > **some + uncountable noun**

---

## Any

We usually use **any** in negative sentences and questions.

### Negative sentences

- I don't have **any money**.
- There aren't **any students** here.
- She doesn't have **any brothers**.

### Questions

- Do you have **any questions**?
- Is there **any water**?
- Are there **any restaurants** nearby?

### Structure

- > **any + plural countable noun**
- > **any + uncountable noun**

---

## Some vs. Any

| Sentence    | Quantifier | Example                 |
| ----------- | ---------- | ----------------------- |
| Affirmative | **some**   | I have some books.      |
| Negative    | **any**    | I don't have any books. |
| Question    | **any**    | Do you have any books?  |

### Compare

- There are **some apples**.
- There aren't **any apples**.
- Are there **any apples**?

---

## Some in Offers and Requests

We can use **some** in offers and requests.

- Would you like **some coffee**?
- Would you like **some cake**?
- Can I have **some water**, please?

Here, **some** is natural because the speaker is offering or requesting a quantity.

---

## Common Mistakes

- ❌ I have **any books**.
- ✅ I have **some books**.
- ❌ I don't have **some money**.
- ✅ I don't have **any money**.
- ❌ Are there **some students**?
- ✅ Are there **any students**?

---

## Quick Summary

- **Some** is usually used in affirmative sentences.
- **Any** is usually used in negative sentences.
- **Any** is usually used in questions.
- Both can be used with plural countable and uncountable nouns.
- **Some** is also common in offers and requests.
