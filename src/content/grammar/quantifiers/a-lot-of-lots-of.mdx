**A lot of** and **lots of** are used to talk about a large quantity or number.

Both can be used with **plural countable nouns** and **uncountable nouns**.

They have essentially the same meaning.

---

## A Lot Of

Use **a lot of** with plural countable nouns.

- I have **a lot of books**.
- There are **a lot of students** here.
- She has **a lot of friends**.

Use **a lot of** with uncountable nouns.

- I drink **a lot of water**.
- He has **a lot of work**.
- We need **a lot of time**.

### Structure

- > **a lot of + plural countable noun**
- > **a lot of + uncountable noun**

---

## Lots Of

**Lots of** has the same basic meaning as **a lot of**.

### With plural countable nouns

- I have **lots of books**.
- There are **lots of people** here.
- She has **lots of friends**.

### With uncountable nouns

- We have **lots of time**.
- He drinks **lots of water**.
- There is **lots of food**.

> **lots of + plural countable noun**
> **lots of + uncountable noun**

---

## A Lot Of vs. Lots Of

There is little difference in meaning.

| Expression   | Meaning               | Example        |
| ------------ | --------------------- | -------------- |
| **a lot of** | large quantity/number | a lot of books |
| **lots of**  | large quantity/number | lots of books  |

**Lots of** can sound slightly more informal, but both are very common.

---

## Countable and Uncountable Nouns

| Noun type        | Example            |
| ---------------- | ------------------ |
| Plural countable | a lot of **cars**  |
| Plural countable | lots of **apples** |
| Uncountable      | a lot of **water** |
| Uncountable      | lots of **money**  |

---

## In Different Sentences

### Affirmative

- I have **a lot of homework**.
- She has **lots of friends**.

### Negative

- I don't have **a lot of free time**.
- We don't have **lots of options**.

### Questions

- Do you have **a lot of work**?
- Are there **lots of people** here?

---

## Common Mistakes

- ❌ a lot **books**
- ✅ a lot **of books**
- ❌ lots **water**
- ✅ lots **of water**
- ❌ a lot of **book**
- ✅ a lot of **books**
- ❌ lots of **moneyes**
- ✅ lots of **money**

---

## Quick Summary

- **A lot of** and **lots of** mean a large quantity or number.
- Both work with plural countable nouns.
- Both work with uncountable nouns.
- **Lots of** is generally a little more informal.
- Remember: **a lot of / lots of + noun**.
