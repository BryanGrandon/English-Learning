**And** is a conjunction used to connect words, phrases, or ideas.

It adds information.

---

## Connecting Words

We can use **and** to connect two or more words.

- I like **apples and bananas**.
- She speaks **English and Spanish**.
- Tom and Anna are friends.
- I have a pen and a notebook.

---

## Connecting Adjectives

**And** can connect adjectives that describe the same person or thing.

- She is **friendly and helpful**.
- The house is **big and beautiful**.
- The lesson is **easy and interesting**.

---

## Connecting Actions

We can use **and** to connect actions.

- I get up **and have** breakfast.
- She reads **and writes** every day.
- We went home **and watched** a movie.

---

## Connecting Sentences

**And** can connect two related ideas.

- I like coffee, **and** my brother likes tea.
- She is tired, **and** she wants to go home.
- I studied English, **and** I practiced pronunciation.

---

## Common Mistakes

- ❌ I like apples **and** I like bananas.
- ✅ I like apples **and** bananas.

The first sentence is grammatically possible, but when the subject and verb are shared, we can make the sentence simpler.

- ❌ She is friendly **and** helpful.
- ✅ She is friendly **and** helpful.

This is already correct because **and** connects two adjectives.

---

## Quick Summary

- **And** adds information.
- It can connect words, phrases, and sentences.
- It is similar to **y** in Spanish.
- Think: **A + B**.
