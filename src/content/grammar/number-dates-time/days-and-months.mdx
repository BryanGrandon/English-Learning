## Days of the Week

There are seven days in a week.

| Day       | Abbreviation |
| --------- | ------------ |
| Monday    | Mon.         |
| Tuesday   | Tue.         |
| Wednesday | Wed.         |
| Thursday  | Thu.         |
| Friday    | Fri.         |
| Saturday  | Sat.         |
| Sunday    | Sun.         |

Days of the week always begin with a **capital letter**.

- #### Examples:
  - I work on **Monday**.
  - We study English on **Tuesday**.
  - She visits her family on **Sunday**.

### Weekdays and Weekend

- #### The **weekdays** are:
  - Monday · Tuesday · Wednesday · Thursday · Friday
- #### The **weekend** is:
  - Saturday · Sunday
- #### Examples:
  - I work on **weekdays**.
  - We relax at the **weekend**.

## Months of the Year

There are twelve months in a year.

| Month     | Abbreviation |
| --------- | ------------ |
| January   | Jan.         |
| February  | Feb.         |
| March     | Mar.         |
| April     | Apr.         |
| May       | May          |
| June      | Jun.         |
| July      | Jul.         |
| August    | Aug.         |
| September | Sep.         |
| October   | Oct.         |
| November  | Nov.         |
| December  | Dec.         |

Months always begin with a **capital letter**.

- #### Examples:
  - My birthday is in **March**.
  - Christmas is in **December**.
  - School starts in **September**.

---

## Talking About Days and Months

Use **on** with days:

> on Monday
> on Friday
> on Sunday

Use **in** with months:

> in January
> in June
> in December

Examples:

- I have class **on Monday**.
- My birthday is **in July**.
- We travel **in December**.

---

## Asking About Days & Months

Use What day...? to ask about a day and What month...? to ask about a month.

### Asking About Days

- What day is it?
  - -> It's Monday.
- What day is the meeting?
  - -> It's on Friday.
- What day is your English class?
  - → It's on Tuesday.

### Asking About Months

- What month is it?
  - -> It's September.
- What month is your birthday?
  - -> It's in May.
- When is your birthday?
  - -> It's in May.

## Common Mistakes

### Days and months need capital letters.

- ❌ monday
- ✅ Monday
- ❌ january
- ✅ January

### Use the correct preposition:

- ❌ My birthday is **on** July.
- ✅ My birthday is **in** July.
- ❌ I work **in** Monday.
- ✅ I work **on** Monday.

---

## Quick Summary

- There are **7 days** in a week.
- There are **12 months** in a year.
- Days and months begin with a **capital letter**.
- Use **on** with days.
- Use **in** with months.
