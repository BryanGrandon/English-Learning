## What Is a Date?

A **date** tells us the day, month, and year of a specific event.

- #### Examples:
  - March 10th, 2026
  - July 4th, 2025
  - December 25th, 2026

---

## Writing Dates

English uses different date formats depending on the variety of English.

### American English (US)

The usual order is: _Month + Day + Year_

- #### Examples:
  - January 5, 2025
  - 10/15/2024

### British English (UK)

The usual order is: _Day + Month + Year_

- #### Examples:
  - 5 January 2025
  - 15/10/2024

Both formats are standard. When writing dates internationally, using the month name can make the date clearer.

---

## Saying

### Dates

Dates are commonly said using **ordinal numbers**.

- #### Examples:
  - October 15th -> It's October the fifteenth (US)
  - 15th October -> It's **the** fifteenth **of** October (UK)

### Years

Years are usually spoken differently from ordinary numbers.

| Year | Spoken                    |
| ---- | ------------------------- |
| 1999 | nineteen ninety-nine      |
| 2005 | two thousand and five     |
| 2018 | two thousand and eighteen |
| 2024 | twenty twenty-four        |

---

## Asking About Dates

### Common questions:

- What's the date today?
- What date is it?

### Possible answers:

- It's the first of June.
- It's June the first.

---

## Dates with Prepositions

Use **on** with a specific date.

- #### Examples:
  - My birthday is **on May 12th**.
  - The meeting is **on June 3rd**.
  - Christmas is **on December 25th**.

Use **in** with only a month or year.

- #### Examples:
  - I was born **in May**.
  - I was born **in 2005**.

---

## Common Mistakes

### Capitalization

- ❌ july 4, 2026
- ✅ July 4, 2026

### Prepositions

- ❌ My birthday is in May 12th.
- ✅ My birthday is on May 12th.

### Cardinal vs. Ordinal Numbers

When saying a date aloud, use an ordinal number.

- July **fourth**
- March **twenty-first**

---

## Quick Summary

- _Capitalize_ the names of _months_.
- Use ordinal numbers when saying dates.
- Use **on** with specific dates.
- Use _ordinal numbers_ when saying dates.
- Dates can be written in different formats.
  - In _British English_, dates often include **the** and **of**.
- Remember that British and American English use _different date formats_.
  - American English commonly uses **month + day + year**.
  - British English commonly uses **day + month + year**.
