## What Are Cardinal Numbers?

**Cardinal numbers** are numbers that tell us **how many people or things there are**.

- #### Examples:
  - one
  - two
  - three
  - ten
  - twenty
  - one hundred

We use cardinal numbers for counting, quantities, ages, prices, phone numbers, and other everyday information.

## Teens Cardinal Numbers

The numbers 13 through 19 follow a simple pattern. They all end in **-teen**, which represents the numbers 13 through 19.

### Forming Teen Numbers

Base number + **_-teen_** = Teen number

- four + **_teen_** → _fourteen_
- six + **_teen_** → _sixteen_
- seven + **_teen_** → _seventeen_

Learn the teen numbers from 13 to 19.

## Tens Cardinal Number

The numbers from 20 to 90 are the multiples of ten in English. Learning them will help you form larger numbers, such as twenty-one, thirty-five, and ninety-nine.

### Forming Tens

The multiples of ten usually end in _-ty_.

Base number + **_-ty_** = Tens number

- six + **_ty_** → _sixty_

## Compound Cardinal Numbers

The numbers from 21 to 99 are formed by combining a multiple of ten with a single-digit number.

### Forming Compound Numbers

Compound numbers from 21 to 99 are formed by combining the tens and the ones with a hyphen (-).

Tens + **_-_** + One = Compound number

- #### _Examples_
  - twenty + **_-_** + one → _twenty-one_
  - thirty + **_-_** + five → _thirty-five_
  - sixty + **_-_** + eight → _sixty-eight_

Learn how to form compound numbers using a hyphen.

## Hundreds, Thousands, Millions

Large numbers in English are formed by combining cardinal numbers with hundred, thousand, and million.

### Combining Large Numbers

Cardinal number + hundred / thousand / million

- one hundred -> 100
- two thousand -> 2.000
- ten million -> 10.000.000

**Hundred**, **thousand**, and **million** do not take **_-s_** when they follow a number.

### Example

| Cardinal       | Number     |
| -------------- | ---------- |
| one hundred    | 100        |
| five hundred   | 500        |
| one thousand   | 1,000      |
| fifty thousand | 50,000     |
| one million    | 1,000,000  |
| ten million    | 10,000,000 |

### Combining Units

Large numbers can combine different units:

- 1,250 -> one thousand two hundred fifty
- 25,400 -> twenty-five thousand four hundred
- 3,500,000 -> three million five hundred thousand

In _British English_, **and** is commonly used before the final part of a number:

- one hundred **and** twenty -> 120
- one thousand two hundred **and** fifty -> 1,250

_American English_ often **omits** and:

- one hundred twenty -> 120
- one thousand two hundred fifty -> 1,250

## Cardinal Numbers in Everyday English

Cardinal numbers are used to talk about age, quantity, prices, phone numbers, and other everyday information.

- #### Example
  - I am **twenty years old**.
  - She is **eighteen years old**.
  - There are **three books**.
  - It costs **twenty dollars**.
  - 421-555-0198 (phone numbers)
    - four two one, five five five, zero one nine eight

## Common Mistakes

### Forty

- ❌ fourty
- ✅ forty

### Hundred

Do not normally add **-s** after a specific number.

- ❌ two hundreds
- ✅ two hundred

### Thousand

- ❌ five thousands
- ✅ five thousand

## Quick Summary

- Cardinal numbers answer **how many?**
- **-teen** numbers include 13–19.
- Tens include twenty, thirty, forty, etc.
- Use a hyphen in numbers such as **twenty-five**.
- Use **hundred** and **thousand** for larger numbers.
