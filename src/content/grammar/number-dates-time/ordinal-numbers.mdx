## What Are Ordinal Numbers?

**Ordinal numbers** show the **position or order** of something.

### Compare:

| Cardinal | Ordinal |
| -------- | ------- |
| one      | first   |
| two      | second  |
| three    | third   |
| four     | fourth  |
| five     | fifth   |

- Cardinal numbers answer **how many?**
- Ordinal numbers answer **which position?**

### Examples:

1. She won **_first_** place in the competition.
2. My birthday is on the **_second_** of May.
3. He is the **_third_** student in the line.
4. We live on the **_fourth_** floor.
5. Today is the **_fifth_** day of the course.

## How to Form Ordinal Numbers

Most ordinal numbers are formed by adding -th to the cardinal number. However, some ordinal numbers are irregular or have spelling changes.

### Cardinal number + -th = Ordinal number

Most ordinal numbers are formed by adding _-th_ to the cardinal number.

- four + **_th_** → _fourth_
- six + **_th_** → _sixth_
- ten + **_th_** → _tenth_

### Exceptions

The first three ordinal numbers are irregular.

| Cardinal | Ordinal |
| -------- | ------- |
| one      | first   |
| two      | second  |
| three    | third   |

### Spelling Changes

Some numbers change their spelling before adding **_-th_**.

| Cardinal | Ordinal |
| -------- | ------- |
| five     | fifth   |
| nine     | ninth   |
| twelve   | twelfth |

Numbers ending in zero change **-y** to **-ieth**.

| Cardinal | Ordinal   |
| -------- | --------- |
| twenty   | twentieth |
| thirty   | thirtieth |
| forty    | fortieth  |

## Compound Ordinals

Compound ordinal numbers are formed by combining a multiple of ten with an ordinal number. Only the last word changes to its ordinal form.

### Tens + hyphen (-) + ordinal number

- twenty-first (21st)
- thirty-second (32nd)
- forty-fifth (45th)
- ninety-ninth (99th)

Learn how to form compound ordinal numbers such as 21st, 32nd, and 43rd.

### Ordinal Numbers (21st-100th)

| Number | Word          | Pronunciation       |
| :----: | :------------ | :------------------ |
|  21st  | twenty-first  | /ˌtwen.ti ˈfɜːst/   |
|  22nd  | twenty-second | /ˌtwen.ti ˈsek.ənd/ |
|  23rd  | twenty-third  | /ˌtwen.ti ˈθɜːd/    |
|  24th  | twenty-fourth | /ˌtwen.ti ˈfɔːrθ/   |
|  31st  | thirty-first  | /ˌθɜː.ti ˈfɜːst/    |
|  42nd  | forty-second  | /ˌfɔː.ti ˈsek.ənd/  |
|  53rd  | fifty-third   | /ˌfɪf.ti ˈθɜːd/     |
| 100th  | one hundredth | /wʌn ˈhʌn.drədθ     |

### remember

When writing _compound ordinal numbers_, **_only the last word changes_** to the ordinal form.

- twenty-one → **twenty-first**
- thirty-two → **thirty-second**
- forty-five → **forty-fifth**

## Ordinal Abbreviations

Ordinal numbers can be written in a short form by adding the last letters of the ordinal word to the number.

### Ordinal Suffixes

description="The suffix usually depends on the last digit of the number.

- 1 → **st** (except **11th**)
- 2 → **nd** (except **12th**)
- 3 → **rd** (except **13th**)
- **_All other numbers_** → **th**

### Comparison

| Ordinal      | Abbreviations |
| ------------ | ------------- |
| first        | 1st           |
| second       | 2nd           |
| third        | 3rd           |
| fourth       | 4th           |
| twenty-first | 21st          |

## Uses of Ordinal Numbers

Ordinal numbers are used to show the order, position, or date of people and things.

### Dates

Use ordinal numbers when saying or writing dates.

- #### Examples
  - April 5th
  - the first of May
  - July 21st
  - October 31st

---

### Position and Ranking

Use ordinal numbers to show someone's position in a race, competition, or list.

- #### Examples
  - She finished first in the race.
  - He is second in the competition.
  - Our team came third.
  - I was the fourth person to arrive.

---

### Floors and Order

Use ordinal numbers to describe the order of things, such as floors, chapters, pages, lessons, or attempts.

- #### Examples
  - She lives on the third floor.
  - Turn to the fifth page.
  - Read the first chapter.
  - This is my second attempt.

## Common Mistakes

- ❌ three-th
- ✅ third
- ❌ five-th
- ✅ fifth
- ❌ twenty-th
- ✅ twentieth

---

## Quick Summary

- Cardinal numbers show **quantity**.
- Ordinal numbers show **position or order**.
- The main irregular forms are **first, second, third**.
- Many ordinal numbers use **-th**.
- Ordinal numbers are commonly used for positions, floors, lessons, and dates.
