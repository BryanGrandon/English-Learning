## What Does "There Are" Mean?

**There are** is used to say that two or more people or things exist or are located somewhere.

### Structure

- **There are + plural noun**

Examples:

- There are two books on the table.
- There are three students in the classroom.
- There are many people in the park.
- There are some apples in the kitchen.

---

## There Are with Plural Nouns

Use **there are** with plural countable nouns.

### Structure

- **There are + plural noun**

Examples:

- There are two cats in the garden.
- There are five chairs in the room.
- There are some books on the desk.
- There are many students at school.

The noun must be plural because **there are** describes more than one person or thing.

---

## There Are with Numbers

**There are** is commonly used with numbers.

### Structure

- **There are + number + plural noun**

Examples:

- There are two bedrooms in the house.
- There are ten students in the class.
- There are three windows in the room.
- There are twenty books on the shelf.

Compare:

- There is **one** book.
- There are **two** books.

---

## Negative Form

To make a negative sentence, use **not** after **are**.

### Structure

- **There are not + plural noun**

The common contraction is:

- **There aren't + plural noun**

Examples:

- There aren't any students in the classroom.
- There aren't two bathrooms in the house.
- There aren't many people here.
- There aren't any books on the table.

### Full and Contracted Forms

| Full form     | Contracted form |
| ------------- | --------------- |
| There are not | There aren't    |

---

## Questions

To make a question, put **are** before **there**.

### Structure

- **Are there + plural noun?**

Examples:

- Are there any restaurants nearby?
- Are there two bathrooms in the house?
- Are there many students in the class?
- Are there any books on the desk?

### Short Answers

| Question                   | Positive        | Negative          |
| -------------------------- | --------------- | ----------------- |
| Are there any books?       | Yes, there are. | No, there aren't. |
| Are there two students?    | Yes, there are. | No, there aren't. |
| Are there any restaurants? | Yes, there are. | No, there aren't. |

---

## There Are with Place

**There are** is often followed by a place expression.

### Structure

- **There are + plural noun + place**

Examples:

- There are two books **on the table**.
- There are many people **in the park**.
- There are three restaurants **near my house**.
- There are some students **in the classroom**.

---

## There Is vs. There Are

The main difference is the number of the noun.

| Singular            | Plural                    |
| ------------------- | ------------------------- |
| There is a book.    | There are two books.      |
| There is a student. | There are three students. |
| There is a chair.   | There are four chairs.    |
| There is an apple.  | There are some apples.    |

### Remember

- **One → There is**
- **Two or more → There are**

---

## Common Mistakes

### Using "there is" with plural nouns

- ❌ There is three books on the table.
- ✅ There are three books on the table.

### Using "there are" with a singular noun

- ❌ There are a student in the classroom.
- ✅ There is a student in the classroom.

### Forgetting the plural ending

- ❌ There are two book on the table.
- ✅ There are two books on the table.

## Quick Summary

- **There are** is used with plural nouns.
- Negative: **There aren't**.
- Question: **Are there...?**
- Short answer: **Yes, there are. / No, there aren't.**
- Use **there are** when there is more than one person or thing.

### Basic Pattern

- **There are + plural noun + place**

**Example:** There are three books on the table.
