The verb do is one of the most common verbs in English.

- Do can be a main verb meaning to perform or complete an action.
- Do can be a helping verb used to make questions and negative sentences in the simple present.

---

## Do as a Main Verb

As a main verb, do means to perform, complete, or carry out an activity.

### Have -> Has

| Subject | Verb | Example               |
| ------- | ---- | --------------------- |
| I       | do   | I do my homework.     |
| You     | do   | You do your work.     |
| He      | does | He does his homework. |
| She     | does | She does the dishes.  |
| It      | does | It does the job.      |
| We      | do   | We do our homework.   |
| They    | do   | They do their work.   |

- #### The basic rule is:
  - I / You / We / They -> do
  - He / She / It -> does

---

## Common Expressions with Do

We commonly use do with activities, tasks, and work.

### Do homework

- I do my homework every day.
- She does her homework after school.

### Do housework

- We do the housework on Saturday.
- He does the housework in the morning.

### Do the dishes

- I do the dishes after dinner.
- She does the dishes every evening.

### Do exercise

- They do exercise every morning.
- He does exercise at home.

---

## Do vs. Does

- Do is used with I, you, we, and they.
- Does is used with he, she, and it.

| Subject | Verb | Example                 |
| ------- | ---- | ----------------------- |
| I       | do   | I do my homework.       |
| You     | do   | You do the work.        |
| He      | does | He does his homework.   |
| She     | does | She does the dishes.    |
| It      | does | It does the job.        |
| We      | do   | We do the work.         |
| They    | do   | They do their homework. |

---

## Do as a Helping Verb

Do is also used as a helping verb in the simple present.

- #### It helps us form:
  - negative sentences
  - questions
  - short answers

When do is used as a helping verb, it does not have its own action meaning.

---

## Do in Negative Sentences

Use do not or does not before the main verb.

- #### Subject + do/does + not + base verb
  - I do not like coffee.
  - You do not play tennis.
  - He does not like tea.
  - She does not work here.
  - They do not live in Chile.

### Contractions

| Full form | Contraction |
| --------- | ----------- |
| do not    | don't       |
| does not  | doesn't     |

- #### Examples
  - I don't like coffee.
  - She doesn't like tea.
  - They don't play football.

after doesn't, use the base form of the main verb.

- ❌ She doesn't likes coffee.
- ✅ She doesn't like coffee.

---

## Do in Questions

Use do or does at the beginning of a simple present question.

- #### Do/Does + subject + base verb + ...?
  - Do you like music?
  - Do they play football?
  - Does he work here?
  - Does she speak English?

After does, use the base form of the main verb.

- ❌ Does she speaks English?
- ✅ Does she speak English?

---

## Short Answers

We can use do and does in short answers.

- #### Do you like English?
  - Yes, I do.
  - No, I don't
- #### Does she speak English?
  - Yes, she does.
  - No, she doesn't

---

## Do as Main Verb vs. Helping Verb

The word do can have two different roles.

### Main Verb

Do describes an action or activity.

- I do my homework.

Here, do is the main verb.

### Helping Verb

Do helps form a question or negative sentence.

- Do you like music?
- I don't like music.

Here, do is a helping verb. The main verb is like.

## Common Mistakes

### 1. Using do instead of does

- ❌ She do her homework.
- ✅ She does her homework.

### 2. Adding -s after does

- ❌ He does his homework every day.

This is correct. But:

- ❌ He does studies English.
- ✅ He does study English.

When does is a helping verb, the main verb stays in its base form.

### 3. Adding -s after doesn't

- ❌ She doesn't likes coffee.
- ✅ She doesn't like coffee.

### 4. Adding -s after does in questions

- ❌ Does he plays football?
- ✅ Does he play football?

---

## Quick Summary

- Do can be a main verb: I do my homework.
- Do can also be a helping verb in the simple present.
- I / You / We / They -> do
- He / She / It -> does
- Use do/does for simple present questions.
- Use don't/doesn't for simple present negatives.
- After does or doesn't, use the base form of the main verb.

### Remember

- Main verb: She does her homework.
- Question: Does she do her homework?
- Negative: She doesn't do her homework.
