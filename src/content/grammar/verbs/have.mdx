The verb have is a common verb in English.
It is mainly used to talk about possession, but it is also used in many common expressions and activities.

In the present tense, have has two forms: **_have · has_**

---

## Have and Has

The form depends on the subject.

| Subject | Verb | Example                 |
| ------- | ---- | ----------------------- |
| I       | have | I have a car.           |
| You     | have | You have a book.        |
| He      | has  | He has a dog.           |
| She     | has  | She has a sister.       |
| It      | has  | It has four legs.       |
| We      | have | We have a house.        |
| They    | have | They have two children. |

- #### The basic rule is:
  - I / You / We / They -> have
  - He / She / It -> has

---

## Have for Possession

We use have to say that someone owns or possesses something.

**_Subject + have/has + noun_**

### Example

- I have a car.
- You have a new phone.
- He has a dog.
- She have a brother.
- We have a house.
- They have two cats

---

## Have for Family and Relationships

We can use have to talk about family members and relationships.

- I have two brothers.
- She has a sister.
- He has three children.
- They have many friends.

---

## Have for Physical Features

Have can describe physical features.

- I have brown eyes.
- She has long hair.
- He has a big nose.
- They have blue eyes.

---

## Have in Common Expressions

The verb have is also used in many common expressions.

- #### Food and Drinks
  - have breakfast
  - have lunch
  - have dinner
  - have a drink
- #### Daily Activities
  - have a shower
  - have a bath
  - have a rest
  - have a break

### Examples

- I have breakfast at 8:00.
- We have dinner at home.
- I have a shower in the morning.
- They have a break at 10:00.

---

## Affirmative Sentences

Use: **_Subject + have/has + ..._**

- I have a car.
- You have a book.
- He has a dog.
- She has a new phone.
- We have two children.
- They have a house.

---

## Negative Sentences

For basic present-tense negatives, use do not or does not with the base form have.

**_Subject + do/does + not + have + ..._**

| Subject | Negative     | Example                  |
| ------- | ------------ | ------------------------ |
| I       | don't have   | I don't have a car.      |
| You     | don't have   | You don't have a ticket. |
| He      | doesn't have | He doesn't have a car.   |
| She     | doesn't have | She doesn't have a dog.  |
| We      | don't have   | We don't have time.      |
| They    | don't have   | They don't have a house. |

Notice that after doesn't, we use have, not has.

- ❌ She doesn't has a car.
- ✅ She doesn't have a car.

---

## Question with Have

For basic present-tense questions, use do or does.

- #### Do/Does + subject + have + ...?
  - Do you have a car?
  - Do they have children?
  - Does he have a dog?
  - Does she have a sister?

Again, after does, use have.

- ❌ Does she has a car?
- ✅ Does she have a car?

---

## Short Answer

Questions with have can use do or does in short answers.

- #### Do you have a car?
  - Yes, I do.
  - No, I don't.
- #### Does she have a dog?
  - Yes, she does.
  - No, he doesn't.

---

## Have vs. Has

Compare the forms:

| Subject | Affirmative | Negative         | Question          |
| ------- | ----------- | ---------------- | ----------------- |
| I       | I have      | I don't have     | Do I have...?     |
| You     | You have    | You don't have   | Do you have...?   |
| He      | He has      | He doesn't have  | Does he have...?  |
| She     | She has     | She doesn't have | Does she have...? |
| We      | We have     | We don't have    | Do we have...?    |
| They    | They have   | They don't have  | Do they have...?  |

---

## Quick Summary

- Have is used to talk about possession and many common activities or expressions.
- I / You / We / They → have
- He / She / It → has
- In negatives, use don't have / doesn't have.
- In questions, use Do/Does + subject + have...?
- After does and doesn't, always use have.

### Remember

- Affirmative: She has a dog.
- Negative: She doesn't have a dog.
- Question: Does she have a dog?
- Short answer: Yes, she does.
