**Imperatives** are used to give **commands, instructions, advice, requests, or warnings**.

The imperative uses the **base form of the verb**. The subject **you** is understood and is usually omitted.

- #### Imperatives are used to give:
  - commands
  - instructions
  - directions
  - advice
  - requests

The imperative uses the base form of the verb.

- Open the door.
- Sit down.
- Listen carefully.

The subject you is usually not written.

---

## The Basic Structure

The basic structure is: **_Verb + ..._**

| Verb   | Example           |
| ------ | ----------------- |
| Open   | Open the door.    |
| Close  | Close the window. |
| Sit    | Sit here.         |
| Stand  | Stand up.         |
| Listen | Listen to me.     |
| Look   | Look at this.     |
| Wait   | Wait here.        |
| Come   | Come with me.     |

- #### Examples
  - Open your book.
  - Read the text.
  - Listen carefully.
  - Come here.

---

## Imperatives and "You"

We normally do not use you in an imperative.

- Open the door.
- Listen to me.
- Sit down.
- Not:
  - You open the door.
  - You listen to me.

However, you can sometimes be used for emphasis or to make it clear who is being addressed.

## Positive Imperatives

A positive imperative uses the base form of the verb.

- #### Verb + ...
  - Open the window.
  - Turn left.
  - Take your book.
  - Write your name.
  - Be careful.

### Common Examples

- Come here.
- Go home.
- Wait a minute.
- Look at the board.
- Read the instructions.
- Write your answer.
- Speak slowly.
- Close the door.
- Take a seat.
- Be quiet.

---

## Negative Imperatives

Use don't + base verb to tell someone not to do something.

- #### Don't + verb
  - Don't run.
  - Don't touch it.
  - Don't open the door.
  - Don't worry.

### Examples

| Positive       | Negative             |
| -------------- | -------------------- |
| Open the door. | Don't open the door. |
| Run.           | Don't run.           |
| Touch it.      | Don't touch it.      |
| Speak loudly.  | Don't speak loudly.  |
| Be late.       | Don't be late.       |

### Important

Use the base form after don't.

- Don't go.
- Don't eat.
- Don't talk.
- Not:
  - Don't goes.
  - Don't eats.
  - Don't talks.

---

## The Imperative with "Be"

The verb be has a special form in the imperative.

Use: **_Be + ..._**

- Be careful.
- Be quiet.
- Be patient.
- Be nice.

For negative imperatives: **_Don't be + ..._**

- Don't be late.
- Don't be afraid.
- Don't be noisy.

## Imperatives for Instructions

Imperatives are very common when giving instructions.

- #### At School
  - Open your book.
  - Read the text.
  - Write your name.
  - Listen carefully.
  - Answer the questions.
- #### At Home
  - Clean your room.
  - Close the window.
  - Turn off the light.
  - Wash the dishes.
  - Put your clothes away.
- #### Everyday Instructions
  - Wait here.
  - Follow me.
  - Look at the map.
  - Turn left.
  - Turn right.

## Imperatives for Directions

Imperatives are also used when giving simple directions.

- Go straight.
- Turn left.
- Turn right.
- Cross the street.
- Stop here.

### Example

- Go straight.
- Turn left at the corner.
- Walk for two minutes.
- Stop at the bank.

---

## Please + Imperative

We can use please to make an imperative more polite.

**_Please + verb_**

- Please sit down.
- Please wait here.
- Please open the window.

We can also put please at the end.

- Sit down, please.
- Wait here, please.
- Close the door, please.

### Compare:

- Close the door.
- Please close the door.

Both are correct, but please makes the request more polite.

---

## Let's + Verb

Use let's + base verb to make a suggestion that includes the speaker and other people.

- #### Let's + verb
  - Let's go.
  - Let's eat.
  - Let's study.
  - Let's play.
  - Let's watch a movie.

### Negative Suggestions

- #### Use let's not + verb.
  - Let's not go.
  - Let's not worry.
  - Let's not talk about it.

### Examples

| Structure        | Example          |
| ---------------- | ---------------- |
| Let's + verb     | Let's go.        |
| Let's + verb     | Let's study.     |
| Let's + verb     | Let's eat.       |
| Let's not + verb | Let's not wait.  |
| Let's not + verb | Let's not worry. |

## Common Mistakes

### 1. Adding -s to the verb

Imperatives use the base form.

- Open the door.
- Opens the door.
- Come here.
- Comes here.

### 2. Using "to"

Do not use to before the verb.

- Open the window.
- To open the window.
- Sit down.
- To sit down.

### 3. Using "don't" with -s

After don't, use the base form.

- Don't run.
- Don't runs.
- Don't open it.
- Don't opens it.

### 4. Forgetting "be"

Use be directly in imperative sentences.

- Be careful.
- Don't be late.
- Not:
  - Are careful.
  - Don't are late.

## Quick Summary

- Use Structure Example
- Positive command Verb Open the door.
- Negative command Don't + verb Don't open the door.
- Polite request Please + verb Please sit down.
- Polite request Verb + please Sit down, please.
- Positive suggestion Let's + verb Let's go.
- Negative suggestion Let's not + verb Let's not go.

### Remember

Imperative = base verb

- Open the door.

Negative imperative = don't + base verb

- Don't open the door.

Suggestion = let's + base verb

- Let's go.
