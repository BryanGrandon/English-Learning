## Verbs in Simple Sentences

A basic sentence can follow this pattern:

- #### Subject + verb + object
  - I read books.
  - She drinks water.
  - They play football.
  - We watch movies.
  - He opens the door.

Some verbs do not need an object:

- #### Subject + verb
  - I sleep.
  - She runs.
  - They work.
  - We study.

---

## The Third-Person -s

In the simple present, verbs usually take -s with he, she, and it.

- #### I / You / We / They → base verb
  - I work every day.
  - They study English.
- #### He / She / It → verb + -s
  - He works every day.
  - She studies English.

### More Examples

| Base verb | He / She / It |
| --------- | ------------- |
| work      | works         |
| play      | plays         |
| read      | reads         |
| eat       | eats          |
| drink     | drinks        |
| live      | lives         |
| want      | wants         |
| need      | needs         |

Some verbs have spelling changes:

- study -> studies
- watch -> watches
- go -> goes
- do -> does

---

## Common Verbs in Questions

We can use do or does to make simple present questions.

- Do you like music?
- Does she work here?
- Do they study English?
- Does he play football?

Remember that the main verb stays in its base form after does.

- ❌ Does she likes music?
- ✅ Does she like music?

---

## Common Verbs in Negative Sentences

We can use don't or doesn't with common verbs.

- I don't like coffee.
- They don't work here.
- She doesn't speak French.
- He doesn't play tennis.

Remember that the main verb stays in its base form after doesn't.

- ❌ He doesn't plays tennis.
- ✅ He doesn't play tennis.

| Verb     | Related verb |

| open     | close        |
| give     | take         |
| ask      | answer       |
| buy      | sell         |
| start    | finish       |
| wake up  | sleep        |

- #### Examples
  - Please open the door and close the window.
  - I go to school and come home at 4:00.
  - She asks a question and he answers it.

## Common Verb Patterns

Some verbs are often followed by specific types of words.

### Verb + noun

- read a book
- drink water
- watch TV
- open the doors

### Verb + to + place

- go to school
- go to work
- come to my house

### Verb + to + verb

- Some common verbs can be followed by to + verb.
- I want to learn English.
- We need to study.
- She likes to read.

---

## Quick Summary

- Common verbs help us talk about everyday actions, routines, and situations.
- Important A1 verbs include go, come, eat, drink, sleep, work, study, like, want, need, read, write, speak, and play.
- In the simple present, he, she, and it usually take -s.
- Use do/does to form simple present questions.
- Use don't/doesn't to form simple present negatives.
- After does or doesn't, use the base form of the main verb.

### Remember

- I study English every day.
- She studies English every day.
- Does she study English?
- She doesn't study French.
