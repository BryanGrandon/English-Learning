The verb be is one of the most important verbs in English.
It is used to talk about who someone is, what something is, where someone or something is, and how someone or something is.

The verb be has three forms in the present: **_am · is · are_**

---

## Form of Be

The form of be depends on the subject.

| Subject | Be  | Example            |
| ------- | --- | ------------------ |
| I       | am  | I am a student.    |
| You     | are | You are my friend. |
| He      | is  | He is tired.       |
| She     | is  | She is happy.      |
| It      | is  | It is cold.        |
| We      | are | We are ready.      |
| They    | are | They are at home.  |

### The Basic Rule

- #### I → am
  - I am happy.
- #### He / She / It → is
  - She is tired.
- #### You / We / They → are
  - They are ready.

---

## Be + Noun

we can use be to identify a person or thing.

**_subject + be + noun_**

- I am student.
- She is a teacher.
- He is my brother.
- They are students.
- This is a book.

Here, **be** connects the subject with information about the subject.

---

## Be + Adjetive

We can use _be_ with an adjetive to describe someone or something.

**_Subject + be + adjective_**

- I am happy.
- She is tired.
- He is hungry.
- We are ready.
- They are busy.

The adjetive describes the subject.

---

## Be + Place

We can use _be_ to say where someone or something is.

**_Subject + be + place_**

- I am at home.
- She is at school.
- He is in the kitchen.
- We are in the park.
- They are at work.

---

## Affirmative Sentences

Use _am_, _is_, or _are_ to make affirmative sentences.

- #### Subject + be + complement
  - I am happy.
  - You are kind.
  - He is tired.
  - She is ready.
  - It is cold.
  - We are friends.
  - They are busy.

### Contractions

In everyday English, we often use contractions.

| Full form | Contraction |
| --------- | ----------- |
| I am      | I'm         |
| You are   | You're      |
| He is     | He's        |
| She is    | She's       |
| It is     | It's        |
| We are    | We're       |
| They are  | They're     |

- #### Example
  - I'm happy.
  - She's a teacher.
  - They're at home.

---

## Negative sentences

To make a negative sentence, add _not_ after am, is, or are.

- #### Subject + be + not
  - I am not tired.
  - She is not a teacher.
  - He is not at home.
  - We are not ready.
  - They are not busy.

### Negative Contractions

| Full form | Contraction |
| --------- | ----------- |
| am not    | I'm not     |
| is not    | isn't       |
| are not   | aren't      |

- #### Examples:
  - I'm not tired.
  - She isn't ready.
  - They aren't at home.

### Avoid This Mistake

Do not use do/does to make negative sentences with be.

- ❌ I don't be tired.
- ✅ I am not tired.
- ❌ She doesn't be happy.
- ✅ She isn't happy.

---

## Question

To make a question with be, put am, is or are before the subject.

- #### Be + subject + ...?
  - Are you a student?
  - Is she happy?
  - Is he at home?
  - Are they ready?
  - Am I late?

### Short answers

We can use _be_ is short answers.

- #### Are you tired?
  - Yes, I am.
  - No, I'm not.
- #### Is she a teacher?
  - Yes, she is.
  - No, she isn't.
- #### Are they ready?
  - Yes, they are.
  - No, they aren't.

---

## Be is Simple Sentences

The verb _be_ cna connect a subject with different types of information.

| Use         | Example          |
| ----------- | ---------------- |
| Identity    | She is a doctor. |
| Description | She is happy.    |
| Location    | She is at home.  |
| Age         | She is twenty.   |
| Condition   | She is tired.    |

---

## Quick Summary

- Be is one of the most common verbs in English.
- In the present, it has three forms: am, is, are.
- I → am
- He / She / It → is
- You / We / They → are
- Use be to talk about identity, descriptions, locations, age, and conditions.
- In questions, put be before the subject.

### Remember

- I am a student.
- She is happy.
- They are at home.
