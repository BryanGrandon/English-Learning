A verb is a word that tells us what someone or something does, is, or has. Verbs are an essential part of English sentences.

---

## What Is a Verb?

A verb is a word that expresses an action, a state, or possession.

### Action Verbs

Action verbs describe something that a person, animal, or thing does.

- run
- eat
- sleep
- work
- read
- play

### Examples

- I read every day.
- She runs in the park.
- They play football.

---

## Verbs Describe Actions

Many verbs describe actions that we can see or do.

| Verb  | Example              |
| ----- | -------------------- |
| eat   | I eat breakfast.     |
| drink | We drink water.      |
| walk  | They walk to school. |
| read  | She reads a book.    |
| write | He writes an email.  |
| play  | We play games.       |

---

## Verbs Can Describe States

Some verbs do not describe physical actions. They describe a state or condition.

The verb be is one of the most common examples.

- I am happy.
- She is tired.
- They are ready.

Other verbs can also describe states:

- I know the answer.
- She likes music.
- They want a new car.

---

## Verbs Can Show Possession

The verb have can show that someone or something possesses or owns something.

- I have a book.
- She has a dog.
- They have a house.

The verb have is also used in many common expressions:

- have breakfast
- have lunch
- have a shower
- have a rest

---

## Verbs in Sentences

A basic English sentence often contains a subject + verb.

### Examples

- I work.
- She runs.
- They study.

The subject tells us who or what the sentence is about. The verb tells us what the subject does or is.

### Subject + Verb

| Subject | Verb  |
| ------- | ----- |
| I       | work  |
| You     | study |
| He      | runs  |
| She     | reads |
| We      | play  |
| They    | sleep |

---

## Verb + Object

Many verbs can be followed by an object.

**_Subject + verb + object_**

- I read a book.
- She eats an apple.
- They play football.
- We watch TV.

The object tells us what or who receives the action.

## Quick Summary

- A verb is a word that expresses an action, state, or possession.
- Action verbs include run, eat, read, and play.
- Be can describe a state: She is happy.
- Have can express possession: He has a car.
- A basic sentence often follows the pattern subject + verb.
- Many sentences use subject + verb + object.

### Remember

- Action: She runs.
- sState: She is happy.
- Possession: She has a dog.
