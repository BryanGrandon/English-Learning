Personal pronouns are words we use instead of nouns to refer to people, animals, or things. They can have different forms depending on their function in a sentence.

In English, personal pronouns can change depending on their function in the sentence. The two main types are subject pronouns and object pronouns.

- Maria is my friend. She likes music.
- I like Maria. I like her.

---

## Subject Pronouns

Subject pronouns are used as the subject of a sentence. They tell us who or what performs the action.

Use a **subject pronoun** when the pronoun performs the action of the verb.

| Subject Pronoun | Usage                                                                        |
| --------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| I               | Use I to talk about yourself.                                                |
| you             | Use you for one person or more than one person.                              |
| he              | Use he for one male person.                                                  |
| she             | Use she for one female person.                                               |
| it              | Use it for a thing, an animal when its sex is not specified, or a situation. |
| we              | Use we for the speaker and one or more other people.                         |
| they            | Use they for two or more people, animals, or things.                         |

### Example

- I study English.
- You speak English.
- He works here.
- She is my sister.
- It is new.
- We live in Chile.
- They are students.

---

## Object Pronouns

Object pronouns are used as the object of a verb or after a preposition. They receive the action or are affected by it.

Use an **object pronoun** when the pronoun receives the action of a verb or follows a preposition.

| Subject | Object | Usage                                                |
| ------- | ------ | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| I       | me     | Me is the object form of I.                          |
| you     | you    | You is the same as both the subject and object form. |
| he      | him    | Him is the object form of he.                        |
| she     | her    | Her is the object form of she.                       |
| it      | it     | It is the same as both the subject and object form.  |
| we      | us     | Us is the object form of we.                         |
| they    | them   | Them is the object form of they.                     |

### Example

- She called **me**.
- I called **you**.
- sI saw **him**.
- We know **her**.
- I bought **it**.
- They invited **us**.
- I saw **them**.

---

## Subject vs Object Pronouns

Subject and object pronouns refer to the same people or things, but they have different functions in a sentence.

| Subject | Object |
| ------- | ------ |
| I       | me     |
| you     | you    |
| he      | him    |
| she     | her    |
| it      | it     |
| we      | us     |
| they    | them   |

### Subject or Object? (Common mistake)

Do not use an object pronoun as the subject of a sentence.

- ❌ Him is my friend.
- ✅ He is my friend.

Do not use a subject pronoun as the object of a verb.

- ❌ She called I.
- ✅ She called me.

---

## Pronouns with Verbs

_Subject pronouns_ normally come **_before_** the verb.

- #### Subject + verb
  - I like coffee.
  - She speaks English.
  - They play football.

_Object pronouns_ normally come **_after_** the verb.

- #### Subject + verb + object
  - I like her.
  - She knows me.
  - They help us.
  - We invited them.

---

## Quick Summary

### Subject Pronouns

- #### I · you · he · she · it · we · they

Use them as the subject of a sentence.

- She likes music.

### Object Pronouns

- #### me · you · him · her · it · us · them

Use them as the object of a verb.

- She likes him.
