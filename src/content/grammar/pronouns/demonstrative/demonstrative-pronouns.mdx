Demonstrative pronouns are words we use to point to or identify people, animals, things, or ideas.
They show whether something is near or far from the speaker and whether it is singular or plural.

They show whether something is near or far from the speaker.

- #### There are four demonstrative pronouns:
  - this
  - that
  - these
  - those

---

## This, That, These, and Those

The correct demonstrative depends on distance and number.

| Distance | Singular | Plural |
| -------- | -------- | ------ |
| Near     | this     | these  |
| Far      | that     | those  |

### Singular

- Use this for one thing that is near.
- Use that for one thing that is far.

### Plural

- Use these for two or more things that are near.
- Use those for two or more things that are far.

---

## This

Use this for one person or thing that is near the speaker.

**_This + is + ..._**

- #### Examples
  - This is my phone.
  - This is a good book.
  - This is my friend.

---

## That

Use that for one person or thing that is far from the speaker.

**_That + is + ..._**

- #### Examples
  - That is my house.
  - That is your car.
  - That is a bird.

---

## These

Use these for two or more people or things that are near the speaker.

**_These + are + ..._**

- #### Examples
  - These are my books.
  - These are my shoes.
  - These are new phones.

---

## Those

Use those for two or more people or things that are far from the speaker.

**_Those + are + ..._**

- #### Examples
  - Those are my books.
  - Those are your shoes.
  - Those are new cars.

---

## This vs. That

Both this and that are singular.

The difference is distance.

| Near               | Far                |
| ------------------ | ------------------ |
| This is my book.   | That is my book.   |
| This is a car.     | That is a car.     |
| This is my friend. | That is my friend. |

---

## These vs. Those

Both these and those are plural.

| Near                  | Far                   |
| --------------------- | --------------------- |
| These are my books.   | Those are my books.   |
| These are cars.       | Those are cars.       |
| These are my friends. | Those are my friends. |

---

## Singular vs. Plural

Remember that demonstrative pronouns also change according to number.

| Singular | Plural |
| -------- | ------ |
| this     | these  |
| that     | those  |

### Examples

- This is a book.
- These are books.
- That is a shoe.
- Those are shoes.
- This is my friend.
- These are my friends.

---

## Demonstrative Pronouns with Be

Use is with singular demonstrative pronouns:

- **This** is my book.
- **That** is your car.

Use are with plural demonstrative pronouns:

- **These** are my books.
- **Those** are your cars.

---

## Questions and Answers

Demonstrative pronouns can be used in questions.

| Type     | Question        | Answer           |
| -------- | --------------- | ---------------- |
| Singular | What is this?   | This is a book.  |
| Singular | What is that?   | That is a car.   |
| Plural   | What are these? | These are shoes. |
| Plural   | What are those? | Those are books. |

---

## Demonstrative Pronouns vs. Demonstrative Determiners

The words this, that, these, and those can also come directly before a noun.

When they stand alone, they function as demonstrative pronouns.

When they come before a noun, they function as demonstrative determiners.

| Pronoun              | Determiner            |
| -------------------- | --------------------- |
| This is my book.     | This book is mine.    |
| That is your car.    | That car is yours.    |
| These are my shoes.  | These shoes are mine. |
| Those are your bags. | Those bags are yours. |

### Compare

- This is my phone. ( Pronoun )
- This phone is mine. ( Determiner )

The word is the same, but its grammatical function is different.

## Common Mistakes

Singular and plural

- ❌ These is my book.
- ✅ These are my books.
- ❌ This are my shoes.
- ✅ These are my shoes.

This and these

- ❌ This are my books.
- ✅ These are my books.

That and those

- ❌ Those is my car.
- ✅ That is my car.

## Quick Summary

| Distance | Singular | Plural |
| -------- | -------- | ------ |
| Near     | this     | these  |
| Far      | that     | those  |

- This → one thing near you
- That → one thing far from you
- These → two or more things near you
- Those → two or more things far from you

### Remember

- This is my book.
- That is your book.
- These are my books.
- Those are your books.
