Possessive adjectives and possessive pronouns show who something belongs to.

They are related, but they have different grammatical functions.

- Possessive adjectives come before a noun.
- Possessive pronouns replace the noun.

---

## Possessive Adjectives

Possessive adjectives show who something belongs to and are always followed by a noun.

| Subject Pronoun | Possessive Adjective |
| --------------- | -------------------- |
| I               | my                   |
| you             | your                 |
| he              | his                  |
| she             | her                  |
| it              | its                  |
| we              | our                  |
| they            | their                |

### Examples

- This is my book.
- Is that your phone?
- That is his car.
- This is her bag.
- The dog is eating its food.
- This is our house.
- Those are their books.

### Basic Pattern

**_Possessive adjective + noun_**

- my + book → my book
- her + phone → her phone
- their + house → their house

A possessive adjective cannot normally stand alone. It needs a noun after it.

---

## Possessive Pronouns

Possessive pronouns also show possession, but they replace the noun.

| Subject Pronoun | Possessive Pronoun |
| --------------- | ------------------ |
| I               | mine               |
| you             | yours              |
| he              | his                |
| she             | hers               |
| we              | ours               |
| they            | theirs             |

There is no commonly used possessive pronoun corresponding to its.

### Examples

- This book is mine.
- Is this phone yours?
- That car is his.
- The bag is hers.
- This house is ours.
- Those books are theirs.

---

## Possessive Adjectives vs. Possessive Pronouns

The main difference is whether a noun follows the possessive form.

| Possessive Adjective | Possessive Pronoun    |
| -------------------- | --------------------- |
| my book              | The book is mine.     |
| your phone           | The phone is yours.   |
| his car              | The car is his.       |
| her bag              | The bag is hers.      |
| our house            | The house is ours.    |
| their books          | The books are theirs. |

### Compare

- This is my book.
- This book is mine.
- That is her bag.
- That bag is hers.
- These are our keys.
- These keys are ours.
- Those are their seats.
- Those seats are theirs.

---

## Possessive Adjectives Need a Noun

Possessive adjectives are used directly before a noun.

- my book
- your car
- his phone
- her name
- our house
- their teacher

Do not use a possessive adjective by itself when a possessive pronoun is needed.

- ❌ This book is my.
- ✅ This book is mine.
- ❌ That car is her.
- ✅ That car is hers.

---

## Possessive Pronouns Replace the Noun

Possessive pronouns are used without the noun they replace.

- This is my book. → This book is mine.
- That is your bag. → That bag is yours.
- This is her phone. → This phone is hers.
- These are our seats. → These seats are ours.

The noun is understood from the context.

---

## Possessive Pronouns After Be

Possessive pronouns commonly appear after the verb be.

- #### Singular
  - This book is mine.
  - That phone is yours.
  - The car is his.
  - The bag is hers.
- #### Plural
  - The house is ours.
  - These books are theirs.

### Compare

| Possessive Adjective   | Possessive Pronoun      |
| ---------------------- | ----------------------- |
| This is my book.       | This book is mine.      |
| That is your phone.    | That phone is yours.    |
| This is her bag.       | This bag is hers.       |
| This is our house.     | This house is ours.     |
| These are their books. | These books are theirs. |

## Common Mistakes

Don't use a possessive adjective without a noun

- ❌ This book is my.
- ✅ This book is mine.
- ❌ The car is her.
- ✅ The car is hers.

### Don't use a possessive pronoun before a noun

Possessive pronouns replace the noun, so they do not normally come directly before one.

- ❌ mine book
- ✅ my book
- ❌ hers bag
- ✅ her bag
- ❌ theirs house
- ✅ their house

---

## Quick Summary

Possessive adjectives come before a noun:

- my book · your car · her phone · our house

Possessive pronouns replace the noun:

- mine · yours · hers · ours

### Compare:

- This is my book.
- This book is mine.
- That is their car.
- That car is theirs.

Possessive adjectives need a noun. Possessive pronouns replace the noun.
