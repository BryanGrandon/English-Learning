The pronoun it is used to talk about a thing, animal, situation, place, time, weather, or idea.
It is usually used as a subject pronoun or object pronoun.

---

## What Does "It" Mean?

It is a third-person singular pronoun.

We use it when we do not use a person's name or another noun.

- The book is interesting. It is interesting.
- I have a phone. It is new.
- Where is the dog? It is outside.

---

## It as a Subject Pronoun

We use it as the subject when we talk about a singular thing, animal, situation, or idea.

| Noun         | Sentence           |
| ------------ | ------------------ |
| the book     | It is interesting. |
| the car      | It is fast.        |
| the dog      | It is hungry.      |
| the computer | It is new.         |

### Examples

- This is my phone. It is new.
- I have a cat. It is very friendly.
- Where is the book? It is on the table.

---

## It as an Object Pronoun

We also use it as an object after a verb or preposition.

- I like it.
- I need it.
- She has it.
- Look at it.
- Put the book on the table. Put it there.

### Compare:

| Subject          | Object         |
| ---------------- | -------------- |
| It is new.       | I like it.     |
| It is expensive. | I want it.     |
| It is beautiful. | She bought it. |

---

## It for Animals

We can use it to refer to an animal when we do not know its sex or when the animal is not being treated as a person.

- Look at that dog. It is cute.
- The bird is in the tree. It is singing.
- I saw a cat. It was sleeping.

When the animal's sex is known or important, he or she can also be used.

- My dog is called Max. He is very friendly.
- My cat is Luna. She is sleeping.

---

## It for Weather

We use it in sentences about the weather.

- It is raining.
- It is sunny.
- It is cold.
- It is hot.
- It is windy.

Here, it does not refer to a specific thing.

## It for Time

We use it when talking about the time.

- It is three o'clock.
- It is half past six.
- It is late.
- It is time to go.

Asking About the Time

- What time is it?
- It is five o'clock.

---

## It for Days and Dates

We can use it when talking about days, dates, and periods of time.

- It is Monday.
- It is my birthday.
- It is January.
- It is the first day of school.

---

## It for Places and Situations

We can use it to refer to a place, situation, or experience that has already been mentioned.

- I visited London. It was beautiful.
- We went to the park. It was very crowded.
- I have a new job. It is interesting.

---

## It in Simple Descriptions

We often use it with be to describe something.

### Pattern

**_It + be + adjective_**

- It is good.
- It is beautiful.
- It is easy.
- It is difficult.
- It is interesting.

### Negative

**_It + be + not + adjective_**

- It is not difficult.
- It isn't expensive.
- It isn't cold.

---

## It with This and That

We can use it to refer back to something introduced with this or that.

- This is my new phone. It is expensive.
- That is my car. It is very old.
- This is a good book. I really like it.

## Common Mistakes

### 1. Repeating the noun

- ❌ The phone is new. The phone is expensive.
- ✅ The phone is new. It is expensive.

### 2. Using "he" or "she" for things

- ❌ My computer is new. He is fast.
- ✅ My computer is new. It is fast.

### 3. Forgetting "it" with weather

- ❌ Is raining.
- ✅ It is raining.

### 4. Forgetting "it" with time

- ❌ Is five o'clock.
- ✅ It is five o'clock.

---

## Quick Summary

| Use       | Example                             |
| --------- | ----------------------------------- |
| Thing     | The phone is new. It is expensive.  |
| Animal    | The dog is hungry. It needs food.   |
| Object    | I like it.                          |
| Weather   | It is raining.                      |
| Time      | It is eight o'clock.                |
| Day       | It is Monday.                       |
| Place     | I visited London. It was beautiful. |
| Situation | It is difficult.                    |

It usually replaces a singular thing, animal, situation, place, or idea.
It is also commonly used with weather, time, and dates.
