Possessive adjectives and possessive pronouns show who something belongs to.

**_Los adjetivos y pronombres posesivos indican a quién pertenece algo._**

They are related, but they have different functions in a sentence.

**_Están relacionados, pero cumplen diferentes funciones en una oración._**

- Possessive adjectives come before a noun.
  - **_Los adjetivos posesivos van antes de un sustantivo._**
- Possessive pronouns replace a noun.
  - **_Los pronombres posesivos reemplazan a un sustantivo._**

---

## Possessive Adjectives

Possessive adjectives show who something belongs to. They always come before a noun.

**_Los adjetivos posesivos indican a quién pertenece algo. Siempre van antes de un sustantivo._**

<div className='table-wrapper'>

| Subject Pronoun | Possessive Adjective |
| --------------- | -------------------- |
| I               | my                   |
| you             | your                 |
| he              | his                  |
| she             | her                  |
| it              | its                  |
| we              | our                  |
| they            | their                |

</div>

- #### Examples
  - This is my book. **_(Este es mi libro)_**.
  - Is that your phone? **_(¿Ese es tu teléfono?)_**.
  - That is his car. **_(Ese es su auto)_**.
  - This is her bag. **_(Este es su bolso)_**.
  - The dog is eating its food. **_(El perro está comiendo su comida)_**.
  - This is our house. **_(Esta es nuestra casa)_**.
  - Those are their books. **_(Esos son sus libros)_**.

### Basic Pattern

_Possessive adjective + noun_

- my + book → my book (mi libro)
- her + phone → her phone (su teléfono)
- their + house → their house (su casa)

A possessive adjective needs a noun after it. It cannot normally be used alone.

**_Un adjetivo posesivo necesita un sustantivo después. Normalmente no puede utilizarse por sí solo._**

## Possessive Pronouns

Possessive pronouns also show possession, but they replace a noun. They are used when we already know what belongs to someone.

**_Los pronombres posesivos también indican posesión, pero reemplazan a un sustantivo. Se utilizan cuando ya sabemos qué le pertenece a alguien._**

<div className='table-wrapper'>

| Subject Pronoun | Possessive Pronoun |
| --------------- | ------------------ |
| I               | mine               |
| you             | yours              |
| he              | his                |
| she             | hers               |
| we              | ours               |
| they            | theirs             |

</div>

There is no commonly used possessive pronoun corresponding to its.

**_No existe un pronombre posesivo de uso habitual que corresponda a its._**

### Examples

- This book is mine. **_(Este libro es mío)_**.
- Is this phone yours? **_(¿Este teléfono es tuyo?)_**.
- That car is his. **_(Ese auto es suyo)_**.
- The bag is hers. **_(El bolso es suyo)_**.
- This house is ours. **_(Esta casa es nuestra)_**.
- Those books are theirs. **_(Esos libros son suyos)_**.

---

## Possessive Adjectives vs. Possessive Pronouns

The main difference is that possessive adjectives come before a noun, while possessive pronouns replace the noun.

**_La principal diferencia es que los adjetivos posesivos van antes de un sustantivo, mientras que los pronombres posesivos lo reemplazan._**

<div className='table-wrapper'>

| Possessive Adjective | Possessive Pronoun |
| -------------------- | ------------------ |
| my book              | mine               |
| your phone           | yours              |
| his car              | his                |
| her bag              | hers               |
| our house            | ours               |
| their books          | theirs             |

</div>

### Compare

- This is my book. **_(Este es mi libro)_**.
- This book is mine. **_(Este libro es mío)_**.
- That is her bag. **_(Ese es su bolso)_**.
- That bag is hers. **_(Ese bolso es suyo)_**.
- These are our keys. **_(Estas son nuestras llaves)_**.
- These keys are ours. **_(Estas llaves son nuestras)_**.
- Those are their seats. **_(Esos son sus asientos)_**.
- Those seats are theirs. **_(Esos asientos son suyos)_**.

Notice that the possessive adjective is followed by a noun, but the possessive pronoun stands alone.

**_Observa que el adjetivo posesivo va seguido de un sustantivo, pero el pronombre posesivo se utiliza por sí solo._**

---

## Possessive Adjectives Need a Noun

Possessive adjectives are always used before a noun.

**_Los adjetivos posesivos siempre se utilizan antes de un sustantivo._**

### Examples

- my book. **_(mi libro)_**.
- your car. **_(tu auto)_**.
- his phone. **_(su teléfono)_**.
- her name. **_(su nombre)_**.
- our house. **_(nuestra casa)_**.
- their teacher. **_(su profesor)_**.

Do not use a possessive adjective alone when you need a possessive pronoun.

**_No utilices un adjetivo posesivo por sí solo cuando necesitas un pronombre posesivo._**

- ❌ This book is my.
- ✅ This book is mine. **_(Este libro es mío)_**.
- ❌ That car is her.
- ✅ That car is hers. **_(Ese auto es suyo)_**.

---

## Possessive Pronouns Replace the Noun

Possessive pronouns are used without the noun they replace. The noun is understood from the context.

**_Los pronombres posesivos se utilizan sin el sustantivo que reemplazan. El sustantivo se entiende por el contexto._**

### Examples

- This is my book. -> This book is mine. **_(Este es mi libro. -> Este libro es mío)_**.
- That is your bag. -> That bag is yours. **_(Ese es tu bolso. -> Ese bolso es tuyo)_**.
- This is her phone. -> This phone is hers. **_(Este es su teléfono. -> Este teléfono es suyo)_**.
- These are our seats. -> These seats are ours. **_(Estos son nuestros asientos. -> Estos asientos son nuestros)_**.

---

## Possessive Pronouns After Be

Possessive pronouns commonly appear after the verb be (am, is, are).

**_Los pronombres posesivos aparecen comúnmente después del verbo be (am, is, are)._**

### Singular

- This book is mine. **_(Este libro es mío)_**.
- That phone is yours. **_(Ese teléfono es tuyo)_**.
- The car is his. **_(El auto es suyo)_**.
- The bag is hers. **_(El bolso es suyo)_**.

### Plural

- The house is ours. **_(La casa es nuestra)_**.
- These books are theirs. **_(Estos libros son suyos)_**.

## Common Mistakes

### 1. Using a Possessive Adjective Without a Noun

- ❌ This book is my.
- ✅ This book is mine. (Este libro es mío).
- ❌ The car is her.
- ✅ The car is hers. (El auto es suyo).

Use a possessive pronoun when there is no noun after the possessive form.

**_Utiliza un pronombre posesivo cuando no hay un sustantivo después de la forma posesiva._**

### 2. Using a Possessive Pronoun Before a Noun

Possessive pronouns replace nouns, so they do not normally come directly before a noun.

**_Los pronombres posesivos reemplazan a los sustantivos, por lo que normalmente no van directamente antes de uno._**

- ❌ mine book
- ✅ my book **_(mi libro)_**
- ❌ hers bag
- ✅ her bag **_(su bolso)_**
- ❌ theirs house
- ✅ their house **_(su casa)_**

### 3. Confusing "Its" and "It's"

Its is a possessive adjective. It's is the contraction of it is or it has.

**_Its es un adjetivo posesivo. It's es la contracción de it is o it has._**

- The dog is eating its food. **_(El perro está comiendo su comida)_**.
- It's a beautiful day. **_(Es un día hermoso)_**.

## Quick Summary

### Possessive Adjectives

_my · your · his · her · its · our · their_

They come before a noun.

**_Van antes de un sustantivo._**

- my book **_(mi libro)_**
- your car **_(tu auto)_**
- her phone **_(su teléfono)_**
- our house **_(nuestra casa)_**

### Possessive Pronouns

_mine · yours · his · hers · ours · theirs_

They replace a noun.

**_Reemplazan a un sustantivo._**

- mine **_(mío/a)_**
- yours **_(tuyo/a)_**
- his **_(suyo)_**
- hers **_(suyo)_**
- ours **_(nuestro/a)_**
- theirs **_(suyo/a)_**

### Remember

Possessive adjectives need a noun. Possessive pronouns replace the noun.

**_Los adjetivos posesivos necesitan un sustantivo. Los pronombres posesivos reemplazan al sustantivo._**

- This is my book. **_(Este es mi libro)_**.
- This book is mine. **_(Este libro es mío)_**.
