The pronoun it is used to refer to a singular thing, animal, place, situation, or idea. It is also used in expressions about weather, time, days, and dates.

**_El pronombre it se utiliza para referirse a una cosa, animal, lugar, situación o idea en singular. También se utiliza en expresiones relacionadas con el clima, la hora, los días y las fechas._**

It can function as a subject pronoun or an object pronoun.

**_It puede funcionar como pronombre de sujeto o pronombre de objeto._**

### Examples

- The book is interesting. It is interesting. (El libro es interesante. Es interesante).
- I have a phone. It is new. (Tengo un teléfono. Es nuevo).
- Where is the dog? It is outside. (¿Dónde está el perro? Está afuera).

---

## What Does "It" Mean?

It is a third-person singular pronoun.

**_It es un pronombre de tercera persona del singular._**

We use it instead of repeating a singular noun when we talk about a thing, animal, or another subject that has already been mentioned.

**_Utilizamos it en lugar de repetir un sustantivo singular cuando hablamos de una cosa, animal u otro elemento que ya hemos mencionado._**

### Examples

- The computer is new. It is fast. (El computador es nuevo. Es rápido).
- I have a book. It is interesting. (Tengo un libro. Es interesante).
- The house is big. It has three bedrooms. (La casa es grande. Tiene tres dormitorios).

---

## It as a Subject Pronoun

We use it as a subject pronoun when it comes before the verb and refers to a singular thing, animal, place, or situation.

**_Utilizamos it como pronombre de sujeto cuando aparece antes del verbo y se refiere a una cosa, animal, lugar o situación en singular._**

<div className='table-wrapper'>

| Noun         | Sentence           |
| ------------ | ------------------ |
| the book     | It is interesting. |
| the car      | It is fast.        |
| the dog      | It is hungry.      |
| the computer | It is new.         |

</div>

### Examples

- This is my phone. It is new. **_(Este es mi teléfono. Es nuevo)_**.
- I have a cat. It is very friendly. **_(Tengo un gato. Es muy amigable)_**.
- Where is the book? It is on the table. **_(¿Dónde está el libro? Está sobre la mesa)_**.

---

## It as an Object Pronoun

We also use it as an object pronoun after a verb or a preposition.

**_También utilizamos it como pronombre de objeto después de un verbo o una preposición._**

- #### Examples
  - I like it. **_(Me gusta)_**.
  - I need it. **_(Lo necesito)_**.
  - She has it. **_(Ella lo tiene)_**.
  - Look at it. **_(Míralo)_**.
  - Put the book on the table. Put it there. **_(Pon el libro sobre la mesa. Ponlo allí)_**.

### Compare:

<div className='table-wrapper'>

| Subject                             | Object                                 |
| ----------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| It is new. **_(Es nuevo)_**.        | I like it. **_(Me gusta)_**.           |
| It is expensive. **_(Es caro)_**.   | I want it. **_(Lo quiero)_**.          |
| It is beautiful. **_(Es bonito)_**. | She bought it. **_(Ella lo compró)_**. |

Remember: it is the same form for both subject and object.

**_Recuerda: it tiene la misma forma cuando funciona como sujeto o como objeto._**

</div>

## It for Animals

We can use it to refer to an animal when we do not know its sex or when we are not treating it as an individual pet.

**_Podemos utilizar it para referirnos a un animal cuando no conocemos su sexo o cuando no lo tratamos como una mascota individual._**

### Examples

- Look at that dog. It is cute. **_(Mira ese perro. Es lindo)_**.
- The bird is in the tree. It is singing. **_(El pájaro está en el árbol. Está cantando)_**.
- I saw a cat. It was sleeping. **_(Vi un gato. Estaba durmiendo)_**.

When we know the animal's sex, especially when talking about a pet, we can also use he or she.

**_Cuando conocemos el sexo del animal, especialmente cuando hablamos de una mascota, también podemos utilizar he o she._**

- My dog is called Max. He is very friendly. **_(Mi perro se llama Max. Es muy amigable)_**.
- My cat is called Luna. She is sleeping. **_(Mi gata se llama Luna. Está durmiendo)_**.

## It for Weather

We use it in expressions about the weather.

**_Utilizamos it en expresiones relacionadas con el clima._**

In these sentences, it does not refer to a specific thing. It is used as an impersonal subject.

**_En estas oraciones, it no se refiere a una cosa específica. Se utiliza como un sujeto impersonal._**

### Examples

- It is raining. (Está lloviendo).
- It is sunny. (Está soleado).
- It is cold. (Hace frío).
- It is hot. (Hace calor).
- It is windy. (Hay viento).

---

## It for Time

We use it when asking about or talking about the time.

**_Utilizamos it cuando preguntamos o hablamos sobre la hora._**

### Examples

- What time is it? (¿Qué hora es?).
- It is three o'clock. (Son las tres en punto).
- It is half past six. (Son las seis y media).
- It is late. (Es tarde).
- It is time to go. (Es hora de irse).

---

## It for Days and Dates

We use it in expressions about days, dates, and periods of time.

**_Utilizamos it en expresiones relacionadas con los días, las fechas y los períodos de tiempo._**

### Examples

- It is Monday. (Es lunes).
- It is January. (Es enero).
- It is my birthday today. (Hoy es mi cumpleaños).
- It is the first day of school. (Es el primer día de clases).

---

## It for Places and Situations

We can use it to refer to a place, situation, or experience that has already been mentioned.

**_Podemos utilizar it para referirnos a un lugar, situación o experiencia que ya hemos mencionado._**

### Examples

- I visited New York. It was beautiful. (Visité New York. Era hermoso).
- We went to the park. It was very crowded. (Fuimos al parque. Estaba muy lleno).
- I have a new job. It is interesting. (Tengo un nuevo trabajo. Es interesante).

---

## It in Simple Descriptions

We often use it with the verb be to describe something.

**_A menudo utilizamos it con el verbo be para describir algo._**

### Affirmative

_It + is + adjective_

- It is good. (Es bueno).
- It is beautiful. (Es bonito).
- It is easy. (Es fácil).
- It is difficult. (Es difícil).
- It is interesting. (Es interesante).

### Negative

_It + is not + adjective_

We can also use the contraction isn't instead of is not.

**_También podemos utilizar la contracción isn't en lugar de is not._**

- It is not difficult. (No es difícil).
- It isn't expensive. (No es caro).
- It isn't cold. (No hace frío).

---

## It with This and That

We can use it to refer back to something introduced with this or that.

**_Podemos utilizar it para volver a referirnos a algo que presentamos anteriormente con this o that._**

### Examples

- This is my new phone. It is expensive. (Este es mi nuevo teléfono. Es caro).
- That is my car. It is very old. (Ese es mi auto. Es muy antiguo).
- This is a good book. I really like it. (Este es un buen libro. Me gusta mucho).

---

## Common Mistakes

1. Repeating the Noun

- ❌ The phone is new. The phone is expensive.
- ✅ The phone is new. It is expensive.

Use it to avoid repeating the same noun.

**_Utiliza it para evitar repetir el mismo sustantivo._**

### 2. Using "He" or "She" for Things

- ❌ My computer is new. He is fast.
- ✅ My computer is new. It is fast.

Use it when referring to things.

**_Utiliza it cuando te refieras a cosas._**

### 3. Forgetting "It" with Weather

- ❌ Is raining.
- ✅ It is raining.

Use it as the subject in weather expressions.

**_Utiliza it como sujeto en las expresiones relacionadas con el clima._**

### 4. Forgetting "It" with Time

- ❌ Is five o'clock.
- ✅ It is five o'clock.

Use it when talking about the time.

**_Utiliza it cuando hables de la hora._**

It is a third-person singular pronoun. It can be used as a subject or an object to refer to singular things, animals, places, situations, and ideas.

**_It es un pronombre de tercera persona del singular. Puede utilizarse como sujeto o como objeto para referirse a cosas, animales, lugares, situaciones e ideas en singular._**

It is also used in expressions about weather, time, days, and dates.

**_También se utiliza en expresiones relacionadas con el clima, la hora, los días y las fechas._**

Use it to avoid repeating a singular noun. In expressions about weather and time, it works as an impersonal subject.

**_Utiliza it para evitar repetir un sustantivo singular. En las expresiones relacionadas con el clima y la hora, it funciona como un sujeto impersonal._**
