Demonstrative pronouns are words we use to point to or identify people, animals, things, or ideas.

**_Los pronombres demostrativos son palabras que utilizamos para señalar o identificar personas, animales, cosas o ideas._**

They show whether something is near or far from the speaker and whether it is singular or plural.

**_Indican si algo está cerca o lejos de quien habla y si es singular o plural._**

There are four main demonstrative pronouns:

**_Existen cuatro pronombres demostrativos principales:_**

- This
- That
- These
- Those

---

## This, That, These, and Those

The correct demonstrative depends on two things: distance and number.

**_El demostrativo correcto depende de dos aspectos: la distancia y el número._**

<div className='table-wrapper'>

| Distance | Singular | Plural |
| -------- | -------- | ------ |
| Near     | this     | these  |
| Far      | that     | those  |

</div>

### Singular

- This refers to one person or thing that is near.
  - **_This se refiere a una persona o cosa que está cerca._**
- That refers to one person or thing that is far away.
  - **_That se refiere a una persona o cosa que está lejos._**

### Plural

- These refers to two or more people or things that are near.
  - **_These se refiere a dos o más personas o cosas que están cerca._**
- Those refers to two or more people or things that are far away.
  - **_Those se refiere a dos o más personas o cosas que están lejos._**

---

## This

Use this to point to one person, animal, or thing that is near the speaker.

**_Utilizamos this para señalar a una persona, animal o cosa que está cerca de quien habla._**

_This + is + ..._

### Examples

- This is my phone. **_(Este es mi teléfono)_**.
- This is a good book. **_(Este es un buen libro)_**.
- This is my friend. **_(Este es mi amigo)_**.
- This is a cute dog. **_(Este es un perro lindo)_**.

---

## That

Use that to point to one person, animal, or thing that is far from the speaker.

**_Utilizamos that para señalar a una persona, animal o cosa que está lejos de quien habla._**

_That + is + ..._

### Examples

- That is my house. **_(Esa es mi casa)_**.
- That is your car. **_(Ese es tu auto)_**.
- That is a bird. **_(Ese es un pájaro)_**.
- That is my teacher. **_(Ese es mi profesor)_**.

---

## These

Use these to point to two or more people, animals, or things that are near the speaker.

**_Utilizamos these para señalar a dos o más personas, animales o cosas que están cerca de quien habla._**

_These + are + ..._

### Examples

- These are my books. **_(Estos son mis libros)_**.
- These are my shoes. **_(Estos son mis zapatos)_**.
- These are new phones. **_(Estos son teléfonos nuevos)_**.
- These are my friends. **_(Estos son mis amigos)_**.

---

## Those

Use those to point to two or more people, animals, or things that are far from the speaker.

**_Utilizamos those para señalar a dos o más personas, animales o cosas que están lejos de quien habla._**

_Those + are + ..._

### Examples

- Those are my books. (Esos son mis libros).
- Those are your shoes. (Esos son tus zapatos).
- Those are new cars. (Esos son autos nuevos).
- Those are my friends. (Esos son mis amigos).

---

## This vs. That

Both this and that are singular. Use this for something near and that for something far.

**_Tanto this como that son singulares. Utilizamos this para algo cercano y that para algo lejano._**

<div className='table-wrapper'>

| Near                                         | Far                                         |
| -------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| This is my book. **_(Este es mi libro)_**.   | That is my book. **_(Ese es mi libro)_**.   |
| This is a car. **_(Este es un auto)_**.      | That is a car. **_(Ese es un auto)_**.      |
| This is my friend. **_(Este es mi amigo)_**. | That is my friend. **_(Ese es mi amigo)_**. |

</div>

---

## These vs. Those

Both these and those are plural. Use these for things near and those for things far away.

**_Tanto these como those son plurales. Utilizamos these para cosas cercanas y those para cosas lejanas._**

<div className='table-wrapper'>

| Near                                                | Far                                                |
| --------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| These are my books. **_(Estos son mis libros)_**.   | Those are my books. **_(Esos son mis libros)_**.   |
| These are cars. **_(Estos son autos)_**.            | Those are cars. **_(Esos son autos)_**.            |
| These are my friends. **_(Estos son mis amigos)_**. | Those are my friends. **_(Esos son mis amigos)_**. |

</div>

---

## Singular vs. Plural

Demonstrative pronouns also change according to the number of people or things we are referring to.

**_Los pronombres demostrativos también cambian según la cantidad de personas o cosas a las que nos referimos._**

<div className='table-wrapper'>

| Singular | Plural |
| -------- | ------ |
| this     | these  |
| that     | those  |

</div>

### Examples

- This is a book. **_(Este es un libro)_**.
- These are books. **_(Estos son libros)_**.
- That is a shoe. **_(Ese es un zapato)_**.
- Those are shoes. **_(Esos son zapatos)_**.
- This is my friend. **_(Este es mi amigo)_**.
- These are my friends. **_(Estos son mis amigos)_**.

---

## Demonstrative Pronouns with Be

Demonstrative pronouns use is or are depending on whether they are singular or plural.

**_Los pronombres demostrativos utilizan is o are dependiendo de si son singulares o plurales._**

### Singular: This and That

Use is with this and that.

**_Utilizamos is con this y that._**

- This is my book. **_(Este es mi libro)_**.
- That is your car. **_(Ese es tu auto)_**.

### Plural: These and Those

Use are with these and those.

**_Utilizamos are con these y those._**

- These are my books. **_(Estos son mis libros)_**.
- Those are your cars. **_(Esos son tus autos)_**.

## Questions and Answers

We can use demonstrative pronouns to ask questions about people or things.

**_Podemos utilizar pronombres demostrativos para hacer preguntas sobre personas o cosas._**

<div className='table-wrapper'>

| Number         | Question                           | Answer                                |
| -------------- | ---------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| Singular, near | What is this? (¿Qué es esto?)      | This is a book. (Este es un libro).   |
| Singular, far  | What is that? (¿Qué es eso?)       | That is a car. (Ese es un auto).      |
| Plural, near   | What are these? (¿Qué son estos?). | These are shoes. (Estos son zapatos). |
| Plural, far    | What are those? (¿Qué son esos?).  | Those are books. (Esos son libros).   |

</div>

### Short Answers

In everyday English, we can also answer with just a noun phrase.

**_En el inglés cotidiano, también podemos responder utilizando solamente una frase nominal._**

- What is this? — A book. **_(¿Qué es esto? — Un libro)_**.
- What are those? — My shoes. **_(¿Qué son esos? — Mis zapatos)_**.

---

## Demonstrative Pronouns vs. Demonstrative Determiners

The words this, that, these, and those can have two different grammatical functions.

**_Las palabras this, that, these y those pueden cumplir dos funciones gramaticales diferentes._**

- When they stand alone and replace a noun, they are demonstrative pronouns.
  - **_Cuando aparecen solas y reemplazan a un sustantivo, son pronombres demostrativos._**
- When they come before a noun, they are demonstrative determiners.
  - **_Cuando van antes de un sustantivo, son determinantes demostrativos._**

<div className='table-wrapper'>

| Demonstrative Pronoun                              | Demonstrative Determiner                              |
| -------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| This is my book. **_(Este es mi libro)_**.         | This book is mine. **_(Este libro es mío)_**.         |
| That is your car. **_(Ese es tu auto)_**.          | That car is yours. **_(Ese auto es tuyo)_**.          |
| These are my shoes. **_(Estos son mis zapatos)_**. | These shoes are mine. **_(Estos zapatos son míos)_**. |
| Those are your bags. **_(Esos son tus bolsos)_**.  | Those bags are yours. **_(Esos bolsos son tuyos)_**.  |

</div>

The word stays the same, but its grammatical function changes.

**_La palabra se mantiene igual, pero su función gramatical cambia._**

## Common Mistakes

### 1. Confusing Singular and Plural

- ❌ These is my book.
- ✅ This is my book. (Este es mi libro).
- ❌ This are my shoes.
- ✅ These are my shoes. (Estos son mis zapatos).

Use this and that with is, and these and those with are.

**_Utiliza this y that con is, y these y those con are._**

### 2. Confusing This and These

- ❌ This are my books.
- ✅ These are my books. (Estos son mis libros).

Use these for two or more nearby things.

**_Utiliza these para dos o más cosas que están cerca._**

### 3. Confusing That and Those

- ❌ Those is my car.
- ✅ That is my car. (Ese es mi auto).

Use that for one distant thing.

**_Utiliza that para una sola cosa que está lejos._**

## Quick Summary

<div className='table-wrapper'>

| Distance | Singular | Plural |
| -------- | -------- | ------ |
| Near     | this     | these  |
| Far      | that     | those  |

</div>

- This -> one person or thing near the speaker.
  - **_This -> una persona o cosa cerca de quien habla._**
- That -> one person or thing far from the speaker.
  - **_That -> una persona o cosa lejos de quien habla._**
- These -> two or more people or things near the speaker.
  - **_These -> dos o más personas o cosas cerca de quien habla._**
- Those -> two or more people or things far from the speaker.
  - **_Those -> dos o más personas o cosas lejos de quien habla._**

### Remember

- This is my book. **_(Este es mi libro)_**.
- That is your book. **_(Ese es tu libro)_**.
- These are my books. **_(Estos son mis libros)_**.
- Those are your books. **_(Esos son tus libros)_**.

This and that are singular. These and those are plural.

**_This y that son singulares. These y those son plurales._**
