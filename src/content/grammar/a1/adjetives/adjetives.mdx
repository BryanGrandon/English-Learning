An adjective is a word that describes or gives more information about a noun.

Adjectives can describe people, animals, places, things, and ideas.

<ContentGrid> 
<LessonBlock type='example' fullWidth>

- a big house
- a red car
- a happy child
- a small dog

</LessonBlock>
<LessonBlock type='example' fullWidth>

- The tall man is my teacher.
- She has a beautiful garden.
- The red ball is on the table.

</LessonBlock>
</ContentGrid>

---

## Basic Adjectives

There are many different adjectives in English. At A1 level, it is useful to start with common adjectives for describing people, animals, places, and things.

<LessonBlock type="note">

| Category    | Common Adjectives              |
| ----------- | ------------------------------ |
| Size        | big, small, large              |
| Age         | young, old, new                |
| Color       | red, blue, green, black, white |
| Appearance  | beautiful, pretty, ugly        |
| Feelings    | happy, sad, angry, tired       |
| Quality     | good, bad, nice                |
| Speed       | fast, slow                     |
| Temperature | hot, cold                      |
| Difficulty  | easy, difficult                |

</LessonBlock>

---

## Position of Adjectives

Adjectives usually come before the noun they describe.

<ContentGrid>
<LessonBlock type='structure'title='Adjective + Noun' fullWidth >

- a red car
- a small house
- an old book
- two happy children

</LessonBlock>
<LessonBlock type='tip' fullWidth>

The adjective comes before the noun, not after it.

- a big house ✅
- a house big ❌

</LessonBlock>
</ContentGrid>

---

## Adjectives with Be

Adjectives can also come after the verb be.

<LessonBlock type='structure'title='Subject + Be + Adjective'>

- I am happy.
- She is tall.
- He is tired.
- They are ready.

</LessonBlock>

<LessonBlock type="tip" title='Forms of Be'>

| Subject | Be  | Example         |
| ------- | --- | --------------- |
| I       | am  | I am happy.     |
| You     | are | You are happy.  |
| He      | is  | He is happy.    |
| She     | is  | She is happy.   |
| It      | is  | It is cold.     |
| We      | are | We are happy.   |
| They    | are | They are happy. |

</LessonBlock>

## Adjectives Do Not Change

English adjectives usually have the same form regardless of gender or number.

### No Gender Changes

The same adjective is used for masculine and feminine nouns.

<ContentGrid> 
<LessonBlock type='example' fullWidth>

- a tall man
- a tall woman
- a happy boy
- a happy girl

</LessonBlock>
</ContentGrid>

---

### No Plural Forms

Adjectives do not normally take a plural -s.

<ContentGrid> 
<LessonBlock type='example' fullWidth>

- a red car
- two red cars
- a small dog
- three small dogs

</LessonBlock>

<LessonBlock type='common_mistake' fullWidth>

- ❌ two reds cars
- ✅ two red cars

</LessonBlock> 
</ContentGrid>

---

## Multiple Adjectives

We can use more than one adjective to describe the same noun.

<LessonBlock type='structure' title='Adjective + Adjective + Noun'>

- a small black book
- a big red car
- a beautiful old house

</LessonBlock>

When several adjectives are used together, they usually follow a specific order.

### Basic Adjective Order

At A1 level, focus on these common categories:

<LessonBlock type="note" >

| Order | Type    | Examples                     |
| ----- | ------- | ---------------------------- |
| 1     | Opinion | beautiful, nice, interesting |
| 2     | Size    | big, small, large            |
| 3     | Age     | old, young, new              |
| 4     | Color   | red, blue, green             |
| 5     | Origin  | Spanish, American            |

</LessonBlock>

## Basic Opposites

Many common adjectives have an opposite with a contrasting meaning.

<LessonBlock type="comparison">

| Adjective | Opposite  |
| --------- | --------- |
| big       | small     |
| tall      | short     |
| long      | short     |
| old       | young     |
| hot       | cold      |
| fast      | slow      |
| happy     | sad       |
| good      | bad       |
| easy      | difficult |
| clean     | dirty     |
| open      | closed    |
| new       | old       |
| strong    | weak      |
| beautiful | ugly      |

</LessonBlock>
