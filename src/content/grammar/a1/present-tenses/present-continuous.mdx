The Present Continuous is used to talk about actions that are happening now, around the present time, or situations that are temporary.

## Affirmative Sentences

The Present Continuous is formed with the verb be + the -ing form of the main verb.

<LessonBlock type='structure' title='Subject + Be + Verb-ing' fullWidth>

- #### I + am + verb-ing
  - I am studying English.
- #### You / We / They + are + verb-ing
  - You are working today.
  - We are watching a movie.
  - They are playing football.
- #### He / She / It + is + verb-ing
  - He is reading a book.
  - She is cooking dinner.
  - It is raining.

</LessonBlock>

<LessonBlock type='rule' title='The -ing Form' fullWidth>

Most verbs form the -ing form by adding -ing to the base verb.

- work → working
- play → playing
- read → reading
- watch → watching

Some verbs have spelling changes:

- make → making
- write → writing
- run → running
- sit → sitting

</LessonBlock>

## Negative Sentences

We make negative sentences by adding not after the verb be.

<LessonBlock type='structure' title='Subject + Be + Not + Verb-ing' fullWidth>

- #### I + am not + verb-ing
  - I am not working today.
- #### You / We / They + are not + verb-ing
  - You aren't listening.
  - We aren't watching TV.
  - They aren't studying.
- #### He / She / It + is not + verb-ing
  - He isn't sleeping.
  - She isn't cooking.
  - It isn't raining.

</LessonBlock>

<LessonBlock type="comparison" title='Contractions'>

| Full form | Contraction |
| --------- | ----------- |
| am not    | I'm not     |
| is not    | isn't       |
| are not   | aren't      |

</LessonBlock>

## Questions

We form Present Continuous questions by putting the verb be before the subject.

<LessonBlock type='structure' title='Be + Subject + Verb-ing?' fullWidth>

- #### Am + I + verb-ing?
  - Am I speaking too fast?
- #### Are + you / we / they + verb-ing?
  - Are you studying?
  - Are they working today?
- #### Is + he / she / it + verb-ing?
  - Is he sleeping?
  - Is she cooking dinner?
  - Is it raining?

</LessonBlock>

---

## Short Answers

We use the verb be in short answers.

<LessonBlock type='note'>

| Question           | Yes            | No               |
| ------------------ | -------------- | ---------------- |
| Are you working?   | Yes, I am.     | No, I'm not.     |
| Are they studying? | Yes, they are. | No, they aren't. |
| Is he sleeping?    | Yes, he is.    | No, he isn't.    |
| Is she cooking?    | Yes, she is.   | No, she isn't.   |

</LessonBlock>

## Wh- Questions

We can use question words with the Present Continuous.

<LessonBlock type='structure' title='Wh- Word + Be + Subject + Verb-ing?' fullWidth>

What / Where / Why / Who + am/is/are + subject + verb-ing?

- What are you doing?
- Where is she going?
- Why are they running?
- Who is he talking to?
- What is it doing?

</LessonBlock>

## Uses

We use the Present Continuous for several common situations.

### 1. Actions Happening Now

We use it for actions that are happening at the moment of speaking.

- I am doing my homework.
- She is talking on the phone.
- They are playing outside.
- We are having dinner.

### 2. Actions Happening Around Now

The action does not have to happen at the exact moment of speaking. It can be happening during the current period.

- I am learning English.
- He is working on a new project.
- They are studying for their exams.

### 3. Temporary Situations

We use it for situations that are temporary rather than permanent.

- I am living with my grandparents this month.
- She is working from home this week.
- We are staying at a hotel.

### 4. Changing Situations

We can use the Present Continuous for situations that are gradually changing.

- The weather is getting colder.
- Your English is improving.
- The city is growing quickly.

---

## Common Time Expressions

The Present Continuous is often used with expressions that refer to the present or a temporary period.

- now
- right now
- at the moment
- today
- this week
- this month
- these days

<LessonBlock type='example'>

- She is studying now.
- We are working this week.
- They are staying here at the moment.
- I'm learning a lot these days.

</LessonBlock>

## Common Mistakes

<ContentGrid>
<LessonBlock type='common_mistake' title='Forgetting the Verb Be' fullWidth>

The Present Continuous always needs a form of be.

- ✅ She is working.
- ❌ She working.

</LessonBlock>

<LessonBlock type='common_mistake' title='Using the Base Verb' fullWidth>

The main verb must use the -ing form.

- ✅ They are playing football.
- ❌ They are play football.

</LessonBlock>
</ContentGrid>

<LessonBlock type='common_mistake' title='Using Do or Does' fullWidth>

Do not use do/does to form Present Continuous questions or negatives.

- ✅ Is he working?
- ❌ Does he working?
- ✅ She isn't working.
- ❌ She doesn't working.

</LessonBlock>
