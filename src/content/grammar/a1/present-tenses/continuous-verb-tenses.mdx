# Continuous Verb Tenses

Continuous tenses describe actions that are:

- Happening now
- In progress
- Temporary

<Grammar type='structure' title='Continuous Structure' highlight='to be + verb + -ing'>

- #### Examples
  - I am studying
  - She is working

</Grammar>

---

## Types of Verbs NOT used in Continuous

### Verbs of Senses

- see
- hear
- smell
- taste
- feel

<Grammar type='common_mistake' title='Senses Verbs'>

- ❌ I am seeing you
- ✅ I see you

</Grammar>

---

### Emotions & Mental States

- love
- like
- hate
- know
- believe
- understands
- want

<Grammar type='common_mistake' title='Emotions & Mental States'>

- ❌ I am knowing him
- ✅ I know him

</Grammar>

---

### State / Abstract Verbs

- be
- need
- seem
- exist
- cost
- contain

<Grammar type='common_mistake' title='State / Abstract Verbs'>

- ❌ He is needing help
- ✅ He needs help

</Grammar>

---

### Possession Verbs

- have (possession)
- own
- belong
- possess

<Grammar type='common_mistake' title='Possession Verbs'>

- ❌ I am having a car
- ✅ I have a car

</Grammar>

---

## Special Cases (Important!)

### Same verb, different meaning

Some verbs CAN be continuous depending on meaning:

<Callout type='note'>

- ❌ I **have** a car → possession
- ✅ I **am having** dinner → action

</Callout>

---

### “To be” in continuous

Usually not used in continuous because it is a state verb, but can be used in continuous to describe temporary behavior.

<Callout type='note'>

- You are silly → personality
- You are being silly → behavior (temporary)

</Callout>
