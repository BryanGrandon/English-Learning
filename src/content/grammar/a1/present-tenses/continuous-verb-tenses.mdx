Continuous verb tenses describe actions that are in progress or happening around a particular time.

They are formed using a form of the verb be followed by a verb ending in -ing.

<LessonBlock type='rule' title='Subject + be + verb-ing' >

- I am studying.
- She is working.
- They are playing.

</LessonBlock>

---

## The verb "be"

The form of be changes depending on the subject and the tense.

<LessonBlock type="definition">

| Tense              | Forms of be   | Example               |
| ------------------ | ------------- | --------------------- |
| Present Continuous | am / is / are | She is studying.      |
| Past Continuous    | was / were    | She was studying.     |
| Future Continuous  | will be       | She will be studying. |

</LessonBlock>

---

## Present Continuous

The Present Continuous describes an action that is happening now or around the present time.

<LessonBlock type='structure' title='Subject + am / is / are + verb-ing'  >

| Subject         | Be  | Example           |
| --------------- | --- | ----------------- |
| I               | am  | I am reading.     |
| You             | are | You are reading.  |
| He / She / It   | is  | She is reading.   |
| We / You / They | are | They are reading. |

- I am doing my homework.
- He is watching TV.
- We are learning English.
- They are playing outside.

</LessonBlock>

### Uses

We commonly use the Present Continuous for:

- Actions happening now.
- Temporary situations.
- Actions happening around the present time.

<LessonBlock type='example' title='Examples'>

- #### Now:
- She is talking on the phone.
- #### Temporary situation:
- I am staying with my grandparents this week.
- #### Around the present:
- They are studying for their exams.

</LessonBlock>

---

## Past Continuous

The Past Continuous describes an action that was in progress at a specific time in the past.

<LessonBlock type="structure" title='Subject + was / were + verb-ing'>

| Subject           | Be   | Example             |
| ----------------- | ---- | ------------------- |
| I / He / She / It | was  | She was sleeping.   |
| You / We / They   | were | They were sleeping. |

- I was studying at 8 p.m.
- She was cooking dinner.
- We were watching a movie.
- They were playing football.

</LessonBlock>

### Uses

We commonly use the Past Continuous for:

- An action in progress at a specific time in the past.
- An action that was interrupted by another action.
- Two actions happening at the same time.

<LessonBlock type='example' title='Examples'>

- #### Specific time:
- At 7 p.m., I was eating dinner.
- #### Interrupted action:
- I was sleeping when the phone rang.
- #### Two actions:
- While I was reading, my brother was watching TV.

</LessonBlock>

---

## Future Continuous

The Future Continuous describes an action that will be in progress at a specific time in the future.

<LessonBlock type='structure' title='Subject + will be + verb-ing'>

The form is the same for all subjects.

| Subject         | Structure          | Example               |
| --------------- | ------------------ | --------------------- |
| I               | will be + verb-ing | I will be working.    |
| He / She / It   | will be + verb-ing | She will be working.  |
| We / You / They | will be + verb-ing | They will be working. |

- I will be studying at 8 p.m.
- She will be traveling tomorrow morning.
- We will be staying at a hotel.
- They will be working next week.

</LessonBlock>

### Uses

We commonly use the Future Continuous for:

- An action that will be in progress at a specific future time.
- An action that will happen as part of a future situation or period.

<LessonBlock type='example' title='Examples'>

- #### Specific future time:
- At 9 p.m., I will be watching TV.
- #### Future period:
- This time next week, we will be traveling.

</LessonBlock>

## Continuous Tenses Comparison

The main difference between continuous tenses is when the action is in progress.

| Tense              | Time                      | Example             |
| ------------------ | ------------------------- | ------------------- |
| Present Continuous | Now / around now          | I am studying.      |
| Past Continuous    | In progress in the past   | I was studying.     |
| Future Continuous  | In progress in the future | I will be studying. |

<LessonBlock type='comparison' title='Compare the Tenses' fullWidth>

- Now: I am studying.
- Yesterday at 8 p.m.: I was studying.
- Tomorrow at 8 p.m.: I will be studying.

The action study is in progress, but the time changes.

</LessonBlock>

## Forming the -ing Form

Continuous tenses use the -ing form of the main verb.

<LessonBlock type='rule' title='Add -ing to the verb.'>

| Verb  | -ing Form |
| ----- | --------- |
| work  | working   |
| play  | playing   |
| read  | reading   |
| study | studying  |

</LessonBlock>

<LessonBlock type="rule" title="Some verbs require spelling changes.">

| Verb  | -ing Form |
| ----- | --------- |
| make  | making    |
| write | writing   |
| run   | running   |
| sit   | sitting   |

</LessonBlock>

<LessonBlock type='remember'>

The continuous form always uses:

- be + verb-ing

The verb be shows the tense, while the -ing form shows that the action is continuous.

- She is working.
- She was working.
- She will be working.

</LessonBlock>

## Negative Forms

To make a continuous tense negative, add not after the verb be.

<LessonBlock type="structure">

| Tense   | Structure                  | Example                  |
| ------- | -------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Present | am/is/are + not + verb-ing | She is not working.      |
| Past    | was/were + not + verb-ing  | She was not working.     |
| Future  | will not be + verb-ing     | She will not be working. |

</LessonBlock>

Common contractions:

- is not → isn't
- are not → aren't
- was not → wasn't
- were not → weren't
- will not → won't

---

## Questions

To form questions, put the auxiliary verb be before the subject.

### Present Continuous

Am / Is / Are + subject + verb-ing?

- Are you studying?
- Is she working?
- Are they playing?

### Past Continuous

Was / Were + subject + verb-ing?

- Was he sleeping?
- Were they studying?

### Future Continuous

Will + subject + be + verb-ing?

- Will you be working tomorrow?
- Will they be traveling next week?
