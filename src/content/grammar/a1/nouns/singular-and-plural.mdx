A noun can be singular or plural. A **singular noun** refers to **_one_** person, place, animal, thing, or idea,
while a **plural noun** refers to **_two or more_** people, places, animals, things, or ideas.

<LessonBlock type='comparison' title='Singular vs. Plural Nouns'>

| Singular | Plural   |
| -------- | -------- |
| book     | books    |
| student  | students |
| car      | cars     |
| apple    | apples   |

</LessonBlock>

---

## How to form plural nouns

<LessonBlock type="rule" >

| Ending                                 | Plural Rule                   | Example         |
| -------------------------------------- | ----------------------------- | --------------- |
| most nouns                             | add **-s**                    | book → books    |
| **-s, -ss, -sh, -ch, -x, -z**          | add **-es**                   | bus → buses     |
| consonant + **y**                      | change **-y** to **-ies**     | party → parties |
| vowel + **y**                          | add **-s**                    | boy → boys      |
| some nouns ending in **-f** or **-fe** | change **-f/-fe** to **-ves** | leaf → leaves   |

</LessonBlock>

---

### 1. Add -s

Most nouns form the plural by adding **-s**.

<ContentGrid columns={3}>
<LessonBlock type='structure' title='Singular + -s' fullWidth >

- _car_ + **_-s_** → **cars**
- _house_ + **_-s_** → **houses**

</LessonBlock>

<LessonBlock type='example' fullWidth>

- I have three **books**.

</LessonBlock>
</ContentGrid>

---

### 2. Add -es

If a noun ends in _s, ss, sh, ch, x, or o_, add **_-es_**.

<ContentGrid>
<LessonBlock type='structure' title='Singular + es' fullWidth>

- _bus_ + **_-es_** → **buses**
- _box_ + **_-es_** → **boxes**

</LessonBlock>

<LessonBlock type='example' fullWidth>
  - There are three **buses** outside.
</LessonBlock>
</ContentGrid>

---

### 3. Nouns ending in -y

Check the letter before **-y**:

- If a _consonant_, change **-y** to **_-ies_**
- If a _vowel_, simply add **_-s_**.

<ContentGrid>
<LessonBlock type='structure' title='Ending in -y' fullWidth>

| Ending            | Plural Rule               |
| ----------------- | ------------------------- |
| consonant + **y** | change **-y** to **-ies** |
| vowel + **y**     | add **-s**                |

</LessonBlock>

<LessonBlock type='example' fullWidth>
  - There are three part***ies*** this weekend.
</LessonBlock>
</ContentGrid>

---

### 4. Nouns ending in -f or -fe

Some nouns ending in **f** or **fe** change to **ves** in the plural.

<ContentGrid>

<LessonBlock type='structure' title='f / fe → ves' fullWidth>

- lea**f** → lea***ves***
- wi**fe** → wi***ves***

</LessonBlock>

<LessonBlock type='example' fullWidth>
  - She has two **knives** in the kitchen.
</LessonBlock>
</ContentGrid>

---

## Irregular plural nouns

Some nouns do **_not_** follow the _regular plural rules_. Their forms change completely.

<LessonBlock type="important">
| Singular | Plural   |
| -------- | -------- |
| man      | men      |
| woman    | women    |
| child    | children |
| person   | people   |
| mouse    | mice     |
| tooth    | teeth    |
| foot     | feet     |

</LessonBlock>

---

## Nouns with the same singular and plural form

Some nouns have the same form in both singular and plural.

<LessonBlock type='remember'>

| Singular | Plural |
| -------- | ------ |
| sheep    | sheep  |
| fish     | fish   |
| deer     | deer   |

</LessonBlock>

---

## Singular and plural with articles

- #### Use **a/an** with _singular countable nouns_.
  - a book
  - a car
  - an apple
  - an orange
- #### Do **_not_** use **a/an** with _plural nouns_.
  - books
  - cars
  - apples
  - oranges

<ContentGrid columns={3}>
<LessonBlock type="example" fullWidth>

- I have a book.
- I have three books.
- She is a teacher.
- They are teachers.

</LessonBlock>
</ContentGrid>

---

## Singular and plural with verbs

Singular and plural nouns can affect the form of the verb be.

- #### Singular
  - The boy is happy.
  - The dog is outside.
  - The book is interesting.
- #### Plural
  - The boys are happy.
  - The dogs are outside.
  - The books are interesting.
