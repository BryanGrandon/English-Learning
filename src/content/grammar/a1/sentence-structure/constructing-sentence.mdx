# Basic sentence structure

A sentence in English follows a clear structure:

<Grammar title='Basic sentence structure' highlight='Subject + Verb + Complement' type='structure'>

- Subject → who does the action
- Verb → action
- Complement → extra information
- #### Examples.
  - She studies English
  - They play soccer

</Grammar>

---

## Affirmative Sentences

Used to express positive statements

<Grammar type='structure' title='Affirmative Sentences' highlight='Subject + Verb + Complement'>

- #### Examples.
  - I like pizza
  - He works in an office
  - We live in Chile

</Grammar>

In affirmative sentences, the subject usually comes before the verb.

---

## Negative Sentences

Used to express something is not true, English usually uses the auxiliary **“do”**.

<Grammar type='structure' title='Negative Sentences' highlight='Subject + do/does + not + base verb'>

- #### Examples.
  - She does not like ice cream.
  - We do not live in Madrid.
  - They do not have a car.

</Grammar>

Short forms:

| Full     | Contraction |
| -------- | ----------- |
| do not   | don't       |
| does not | doesn't     |

The **main verb stays in the base form**.

---

## Special Case: To Be / Have Got

With **to be** and **have got**, we _do not use “do/does”_.

<ContentGrid>
<Grammar type="structure" title="To Be" highlight="Subject + am/is/are + not">

- #### Examples.
  - I am not happy.
  - They have not got a car.

</Grammar>

<Grammar type="structure" title="Have Got" highlight="Subject + have/has + not got">

- #### Examples.
  - I have not got a car.
  - He has not got a dog.

</Grammar>
</ContentGrid>

---

## Interrogative Sentences (Questions)

Questions usually begin with an **auxiliary verb**.

<Grammar type="structure" title="Interrogative Sentences" highlight="Auxiliary verb + subject + verb">

- #### Examples.
  - Do you like pizza?
  - Does she work here?

</Grammar>

In questions, the _verb comes before the subject_.

---

## Questions with To Be / Have Got

<ContentGrid>
<Grammar type="structure" title="To Be" highlight="Am/Is/Are + subject">

- #### Examples.
  - Are you tired?
  - Is she happy?

</Grammar>

<Grammar type="structure" title="Have Got" highlight="Have/Has + subject + got">

- #### Examples.
  - Have you got a car?
  - Has he got a dog?

</Grammar>
</ContentGrid>
