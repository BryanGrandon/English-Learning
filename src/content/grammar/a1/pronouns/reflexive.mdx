Reflexive pronouns are used when the subject and the object of a sentence refer to the same person or thing.

The reflexive pronouns are myself, yourself, himself, herself, itself, ourselves, yourselves, and themselves.

<LessonBlock type='comparison' title='Reflexive Pronouns' >

| Subject Pronoun | Reflexive Pronoun |
| --------------- | ----------------- |
| I               | myself            |
| you             | yourself          |
| he              | himself           |
| she             | herself           |
| it              | itself            |
| we              | ourselves         |
| you             | yourselves        |
| they            | themselves        |

</LessonBlock>

---

## Reflexive Pronouns as Objects

Use a reflexive pronoun when the subject does something to itself.

<LessonBlock type='structure' title='Subject + Verb + Reflexive Pronoun' >

- I hurt myself.
- You introduced yourself.
- He taught himself English.
- She looked at herself in the mirror.
- The cat cleaned itself.
- We prepared ourselves.
- They enjoyed themselves.

</LessonBlock>

The subject and the reflexive pronoun refer to the same person or thing:

- I hurt myself. → I = myself
- She looked at herself. → she = herself
- They enjoyed themselves. → they = themselves

---

## Reflexive Pronouns for Emphasis

Reflexive pronouns can also be used to emphasize who performed an action.

<LessonBlock type='example' title='Emphasis' >

- I made it myself.
- She cooked the dinner herself.
- He fixed the computer himself.
- We painted the room ourselves.

</LessonBlock>

Here, the reflexive pronoun does not receive the action. It emphasizes that the person personally performed the action

---

## By + Reflexive Pronoun

Use by + reflexive pronoun to say that someone does something alone or without help.

<LessonBlock type='structure' title='by + Reflexive Pronoun' >
- I did it by myself.
- She lives by herself.
- He traveled by himself.
- They completed the project by themselves.

</LessonBlock>
