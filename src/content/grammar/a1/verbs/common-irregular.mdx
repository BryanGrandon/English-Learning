Irregular verbs are verbs that do not follow the regular -ed pattern when forming the past tense.

<LessonBlock type='example'>

- work → worked → regular
- go → went → irregular

</LessonBlock>

There are many irregular verbs in English, but some are especially common.

---

## Common Irregular Verbs

| Base form | Past simple | Translation         |
| --------- | ----------- | ------------------- |
| be        | was / were  | ser / estar         |
| have      | had         | tener               |
| do        | did         | hacer               |
| go        | went        | ir                  |
| come      | came        | venir               |
| get       | got         | obtener / conseguir |
| make      | made        | hacer / fabricar    |
| take      | took        | tomar / llevar      |
| see       | saw         | ver                 |
| know      | knew        | saber / conocer     |
| think     | thought     | pensar              |
| say       | said        | decir               |
| give      | gave        | dar                 |
| find      | found       | encontrar           |
| eat       | ate         | comer               |
| drink     | drank       | beber               |

<LessonBlock type='important' fullWidth>

Irregular verbs must be learned individually because they do not follow one single past-tense rule.

</LessonBlock>

### Examples

- I went to school yesterday.
- She had breakfast at 8:00.
- We saw a movie last night.
- He made a cake.
- They came home late.
- I ate an apple.

<LessonBlock type='common_mistake' title='Avoid This Mistake' fullWidth>

Do not add -ed to irregular verbs.

- ❌ go → goed
- ✅ go → went
- ❌ eat → eated
- ✅ eat → ate

</LessonBlock>
