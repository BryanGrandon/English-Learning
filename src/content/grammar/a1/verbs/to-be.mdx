# The Verb "To Be"

The verb **to be** means **ser / estar** in Spanish and is **one of the most important verbs in English**. It can be used as a **main verb** or as an **auxiliary verb**.

<Callout type='example'>

- He **is** my friend.
- They **were** at school.
- The book **is** on the table.

</Callout>

---

## Affirmative Form (Present Simple)

| Subject | Verb | Short Form |
| ------- | ---- | ---------- |
| I       | am   | I'm        |
| You     | are  | You're     |
| He      | is   | He's       |
| She     | is   | She's      |
| It      | is   | It's       |
| We      | are  | We're      |
| They    | are  | They're    |

<Callout type='example'>

- I **am** a student.
- She **is** happy.
- They **are** at home.

</Callout>

---

## Negative Form

| Subject | Verb    | Short Form                |
| ------- | ------- | ------------------------- |
| I       | am not  | I'm not                   |
| You     | are not | You're not / You aren't   |
| He      | is not  | He's not / He isn't       |
| She     | is not  | She's not / She isn't     |
| It      | is not  | It's not / It isn't       |
| We      | are not | We're not / We aren't     |
| They    | are not | They're not / They aren't |

<Callout type='example'>

- I **am not tired**.
- She **is not sad**.
- They **are not at home**.

</Callout>

---

## Interrogative Form

In questions with **to be**, the **verb comes before the subject**.

Question

- Am I?
- Are you?
- Is he?
- Is she?
- Is it?
- Are we?
- Are they?

<Callout type='example'>

- **Are you tired?**
- **Is she a teacher?**
- **Are they at home?**

</Callout>

---

## Uses of To Be

<ContentGrid>

<SectionCard title='With Nouns'>

- Used to describe identity or profession
- #### Examples
  - I **am a teacher**.
  - She **is a doctor**.
  - They **are students**.

</SectionCard>

<SectionCard title='With Adjectives'>

- Used to describe states or characteristics
- #### Examples
  - I **am happy**.
  - She **is tired**.
  - They **are hungry**.

</SectionCard>

<SectionCard title='With Prepositions of Place'>

- Used to describe location
- #### Examples
  - The book **is on the table**.
  - The cat **is under the bed**.
  - We **are in the classroom**.

</SectionCard>

<SectionCard title='As an Auxiliary Verb'>

- Used to form verb tenses like Present Continuous
- #### Examples
  - She **is studying**.
  - We **are working**.

</SectionCard>

</ContentGrid>
