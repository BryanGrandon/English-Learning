# Verbs

A **verb** is the part of a sentence that expresses an **action**, **state**, or **existence** of the subject.

<Callout type='example'>

- I **run** every morning.
- She **is** happy.
- They **have** a car.

</Callout>

In these sentences, the verbs show **what the subject does or is**.

---

## Types of Verbs

<ContentGrid>

<SectionCard title='Action Verbs'>

- Describe an action that someone or something performs
- #### Examples
  - run
  - eat
  - study
  - play

</SectionCard>

<SectionCard title='State Verbs'>

- Describe a state or condition, not a physical action
- #### Examples
  - be
  - know
  - like
  - believe

</SectionCard>

<SectionCard title='Auxiliary Verbs'>

- Help form verb tenses, negatives, or questions
- #### Examples
  - be
  - do
  - have

</SectionCard>

<SectionCard title='Modal Verbs'>

- Express ability, possibility, permission, or obligation
- #### Examples
  - can
  - could
  - may
  - might
  - must
  - should
  - will
  - would

</SectionCard>

</ContentGrid>

---

## Verb Position in Sentences

In English, verbs usually appear **after the subject**.

<Grammar type='structure' title='Verb position' highlight='subject + verb + object' >

- #### Examples.
  - She **likes** coffee.
  - They **play** football.
  - I **study** English.

</Grammar>

---

## Short Forms (Contractions)

English commonly uses **short forms** instead of the full verb form.

| Full Form | Short Form |
| --------- | ---------- |
| I am      | I'm        |
| You are   | You're     |
| He is     | He's       |
| She is    | She's      |
| We are    | We're      |
| They are  | They're    |

<Callout type='example'>

- I'm happy.
- She's my friend.

</Callout>
