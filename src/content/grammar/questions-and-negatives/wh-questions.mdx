WH-questions are questions that ask for specific information. They usually begin with a question word such as **what**, **where**, **when**, **who**, **why**, or **how**.

Unlike Yes/No questions, they normally require more than a simple **yes** or **no** answer.

---

## Common Question Words

| Question word | Meaning             | Example            |
| ------------- | ------------------- | ------------------ |
| **What**      | thing / information | What is your name? |
| **Where**     | place               | Where do you live? |
| **When**      | time                | When do you work?  |
| **Who**       | person              | Who is she?        |
| **Why**       | reason              | Why are you here?  |
| **How**       | manner / condition  | How are you?       |

---

## Questions with Be

Use this structure:

- **WH-word + be + subject + ...?**

Examples:

- What is your name?
- Where are you from?
- Who is that?
- Why are they here?
- How is your brother?

### Examples

- #### **What**
  - What is this?
- #### **Where**
  - Where is my phone?
- #### **Who**
  - Who is your teacher?
- #### **Why**
  - Why are you tired?
- #### **How**
  - How are you?

---

## Questions with Do and Does

When the main verb is not **be**, use **do** or **does**.

Structure:

- **WH-word + do/does + subject + base verb?**

### Examples:

- Where do you live?
- What do they eat?
- When do you study?
- Where does she work?
- What does he want?

Remember that the main verb stays in the **base form**.

- Where does she **live**? ✓
- Where does she **lives**? ✗

---

## Questions with Can

Use **can** after the question word.

Structure:

- **WH-word + can + subject + base verb?**

### Examples:

- What can you do?
- Where can I sit?
- When can we meet?
- How can I help you?

---

## What, Where, When, Who, Why, and How

### What

Use **what** to ask about things or information.

- What is your name?
- What do you want?

### Where

Use **where** to ask about places.

- Where are you?
- Where does he work?

### When

Use **when** to ask about time.

- When is your birthday?
- When do they arrive?

### Who

Use **who** to ask about people.

- Who is she?
- Who do you live with?

### Why

Use **why** to ask for a reason.

- Why are you late?
- Why do you study English?

### How

Use **how** to ask about the way something happens or someone's condition.

- How are you?
- How do you go to school?

---

## Common Mistake

Remember the difference between **what** and **where**.

- What is your name? ✓
- Where is your name? ✗

Also remember to use the base verb after **do** and **does**.

- What does he do? ✓
- What does he does? ✗

---

## Quick Summary

- **What** → things or information
- **Where** → places
- **When** → time
- **Who** → people
- **Why** → reasons
- **How** → manner or condition

### Common structures:

- **WH + be + subject?**
- **WH + do/does + subject + base verb?**
- **WH + can + subject + base verb?**
