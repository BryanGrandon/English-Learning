# Yes/No Questions

Yes/No questions are questions that can be answered with **yes** or **no**.

They are very common in everyday English.

---

## Basic Structure

With the verb **be**, put the verb before the subject:

- **Be + subject + ...?**

With most other verbs, use **do** or **does**:

- **Do/Does + subject + base verb + ...?**

---

## Questions with Be

The verb **be** changes position in a question.

### Affirmative

- You are a student.

### Question

- Are you a student?

More examples:

- She is happy.
  - **Is she happy?**
- They are at home.
  - **Are they at home?**
- He is your brother.
  - **Is he your brother?**

### Short Answers

For questions with **be**, use **yes/no + subject + be**.

| Question          | Yes            | No               |
| ----------------- | -------------- | ---------------- |
| Are you tired?    | Yes, I am.     | No, I'm not.     |
| Is she a teacher? | Yes, she is.   | No, she isn't.   |
| Are they ready?   | Yes, they are. | No, they aren't. |

---

## Questions with Do

Use **do** with:

- I
- you
- we
- they

Structure:

- **Do + subject + base verb?**

Examples:

- Do you speak English?
- Do they live here?
- Do we need a ticket?

Notice that the main verb stays in the **base form**.

- Do you **like** coffee?
- Not: Do you **likes** coffee?

---

## Questions with Does

Use **does** with:

- he
- she
- it

Structure:

- **Does + subject + base verb?**

Examples:

- Does he work here?
- Does she speak English?
- Does it work?

The main verb also stays in the **base form**.

- Does she **like** music?
- Not: Does she **likes** music?

### Short Answers

| Question               | Yes            | No               |
| ---------------------- | -------------- | ---------------- |
| Do you like tea?       | Yes, I do.     | No, I don't.     |
| Do they work here?     | Yes, they do.  | No, they don't.  |
| Does he play football? | Yes, he does.  | No, he doesn't.  |
| Does she live here?    | Yes, she does. | No, she doesn't. |

---

## Questions with Modal Can

With **can**, put **can** before the subject.

Structure:

- **Can + subject + base verb?**

Examples:

- Can you swim?
- Can she drive?
- Can they come?

Short answers:

- Yes, I can.
- No, I can't.

---

## Common Mistake

Do not use **do/does** together with **be**.

- Is she happy? ✓
- Does she be happy? ✗

Also, do not use **-s** after **does**.

- Does he work here? ✓
- Does he works here? ✗

## Quick Summary

- **Be:** Be + subject + ...?
- **Do:** Do + I/you/we/they + base verb?
- **Does:** Does + he/she/it + base verb?
- **Can:** Can + subject + base verb?
- Short answers normally repeat the auxiliary verb.

* **Are you ready? — Yes, I am.**
* **Do you work here? — No, I don't.**
* **Does she speak English? — Yes, she does.**
* **Can he swim? — No, he can't.**
