The **Present Simple** is one of the most common verb tenses in English. It is mainly used to talk about **habits, routines, facts, and things that are generally true**.

---

## When to Use the Present Simple

### Habits and Routines

Use the Present Simple for things that happen regularly.

- I wake up at 7 o'clock.
- She goes to school every day.
- We play football on Saturdays.

Common expressions:

- every day
- every week
- every morning
- on Mondays
- usually
- always
- sometimes
- never

### Facts and General Truths

Use it for facts or things that are generally true.

- Water boils at 100°C.
- The Earth goes around the Sun.
- Birds have wings.

### Likes, Dislikes, and Preferences

Use it to talk about things you like or don't like.

- I like music.
- She loves chocolate.
- They don't like coffee.

### Permanent or Regular Situations

Use it for situations that are stable or happen regularly.

- I live in Chile.
- He works in a hospital.
- They study English.

---

## Affirmative Sentences

The basic structure is:

**Subject + base verb + complement**

- #### Examples:
  - I work every day.
  - You speak English.
  - We live in Chile.
  - They play tennis.

With **he, she, and it**, the verb usually takes **-s** or **-es**.

- He works every day.
- She speaks English.
- It rains a lot here.

### Basic Structure

| Subject | Verb  | Example         |
| ------- | ----- | --------------- |
| I       | work  | I work here.    |
| You     | work  | You work here.  |
| We      | work  | We work here.   |
| They    | work  | They work here. |
| He      | works | He works here.  |
| She     | works | She works here. |
| It      | works | It works well.  |

---

## Third-Person Singular

For **he, she, and it**, the verb changes in the Present Simple.

### Most Verbs: Add -s

- work -> works
- play -> plays
- read -> reads
- eat -> eats

Examples:

- He works at home.
- She plays tennis.
- It looks good.

### Verbs Ending in -s, -sh, -ch, -x, or -o

Usually add **-es**.

- watch -> watches
- wash -> washes
- go -> goes
- fix -> fixes
- pass -> passes

Examples:

- She watches TV.
- He goes to school.
- She washes her hands.

### Verbs Ending in Consonant + y

Change **y** to **i** and add **-es**.

- study -> studies
- try -> tries
- carry -> carries

Examples:

- He studies English.
- She tries again.

### Verbs Ending in Vowel + y

Simply add **-s**.

- play -> plays
- enjoy -> enjoys
- stay -> stays

Examples:

- He plays football.
- She enjoys music.

## Negative Sentences

Use **do not (don't)** or **does not (doesn't)** to make negative sentences.

### Structure

**Subject + do/does + not + base verb + complement**

Use:

- **don't** with I, you, we, they
- **doesn't** with he, she, it

Examples:

- I don't work on Sundays.
- You don't speak French.
- We don't live here.
- They don't play tennis.
- He doesn't work on Sundays.
- She doesn't speak French.
- It doesn't work.

### Important

After **doesn't**, use the **base form** of the verb.

- ❌ She doesn't works here.
- ✅ She doesn't work here.

The **-s** moves from the main verb to **does**.

---

## Questions

Use **do** or **does** to make Present Simple questions.

### Structure

**Do/Does + subject + base verb + complement?**

Examples:

- Do you speak English?
- Do they live here?
- Does he work here?
- Does she play tennis?

### Questions with Do

Use **do** with:

- I
- you
- we
- they

Examples:

- Do you like music?
- Do they study English?
- Do we need this?

### Questions with Does

Use **does** with:

- he
- she
- it

Examples:

- Does he work here?
- Does she speak English?
- Does it work?

### Important

After **does**, use the **base form** of the verb.

- ❌ Does she works here?
- ✅ Does she work here?

---

## Short Answers

We can use short answers with **do** and **does**.

### Yes

- Do you speak English?
  **Yes, I do.**

- Does she work here?
  **Yes, she does.**

### No

- Do you speak French?
  **No, I don't.**

- Does he live here?
  **No, he doesn't.**

### Pattern

| Question     | Positive       | Negative         |
| ------------ | -------------- | ---------------- |
| Do you...?   | Yes, I do.     | No, I don't.     |
| Do they...?  | Yes, they do.  | No, they don't.  |
| Does he...?  | Yes, he does.  | No, he doesn't.  |
| Does she...? | Yes, she does. | No, she doesn't. |
| Does it...?  | Yes, it does.  | No, it doesn't.  |

## 7. Wh- Questions

We can use question words with the Present Simple.

Common question words:

- **What** — things
- **Where** — places
- **When** — time
- **Why** — reason
- **Who** — people
- **How** — manner or condition

### Structure

**Wh-word + do/does + subject + base verb?**

- #### Examples:
  - What do you do?
  - Where do you live?
  - When do they study?
  - Why does he work here?
  - Where does she live?
  - What does he like?

## 8. Adverbs of Frequency

Adverbs of frequency are commonly used with the Present Simple to describe how often something happens.

- #### Common adverbs:
  - always
  - usually
  - often
  - sometimes
  - rarely
  - never

Examples:

- I always drink coffee in the morning.
- She usually walks to school.
- They often play football.
- He sometimes works at night.
- We never eat breakfast at school.

### Position

The adverb usually comes **before the main verb**.

**Subject + adverb + verb**

- I always study English.
- She usually works here.
- They never eat meat.

With the verb **be**, the adverb usually comes **after be**.

- I am always happy.
- She is usually tired.
- They are never late.

## 9. Common Time Expressions

The Present Simple is often used with expressions that show when or how often something happens.

### Frequency

- always
- usually
- often
- sometimes
- rarely
- never

### Regular Time

- every day
- every week
- every month
- every year
- on Mondays
- in the morning
- at night

Examples:

- I study English every day.
- She works on Mondays.
- They play football every weekend.
- He usually gets up early.

## 10. Present Simple vs. Present Continuous

The Present Simple is generally used for **habits and regular actions**, while the Present Continuous is generally used for **actions happening around now**.

| Present Simple                   | Present Continuous             |
| -------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| I work every day.                | I am working now.              |
| She studies English.             | She is studying now.           |
| They play football on Saturdays. | They are playing football now. |

Compare:

- I **watch** TV every evening.
  -> This is a routine.

- I **am watching** TV now.
  -> This is happening now.

## Common Mistakes

### Forgetting -s with he, she, or it

- ❌ He work every day.
- ✅ He works every day.

### Using -s after does

- ❌ Does she likes coffee?
- ✅ Does she like coffee?

### Using -s after doesn't

- ❌ He doesn't works here.
- ✅ He doesn't work here.

### Using the wrong auxiliary

- ❌ Does they live here?
- ✅ Do they live here?

### Incorrect position of frequency adverbs

- ❌ I study always English.
- ✅ I always study English.
