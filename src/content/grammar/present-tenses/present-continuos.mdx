The **Present Continuous** is used to talk about **actions happening now, temporary situations, and things happening around the present time**.

The structure is:

**Subject + am/is/are + verb-ing**

---

## Uses

### Actions Happening Now

Use the Present Continuous for an action happening at the moment of speaking.

- I am studying English.
- She is reading a book.
- They are playing football.

### Temporary Situations

Use it for situations that are happening for a limited period of time.

- I am staying with my friend this week.
- He is working in London this month.
- They are studying for an exam.

### Actions Happening Around Now

The action does not have to happen exactly at this moment.

- I am learning English.
- She is reading a new book.
- We are working on a project.

---

## Affirmative

**Subject + am/is/are + verb-ing**

| Subject | Be  | Verb-ing | Example           |
| ------- | --- | -------- | ----------------- |
| I       | am  | working  | I am working.     |
| You     | are | studying | You are studying. |
| He      | is  | sleeping | He is sleeping.   |
| She     | is  | reading  | She is reading.   |
| It      | is  | raining  | It is raining.    |
| We      | are | cooking  | We are cooking.   |
| They    | are | playing  | They are playing. |

### Contractions

- I am → **I'm**
- You are → **You're**
- He is → **He's**
- She is → **She's**
- It is → **It's**
- We are → **We're**
- They are → **They're**

### Examples:

- I'm studying.
- She's working.
- They're playing.

---

## Adding -ing

Most verbs simply take **-ing**.

- work → working
- play → playing
- read → reading
- talk → talking

Some common spelling changes:

### Verbs Ending in -e

Remove **e** before adding **-ing**.

- make → making
- write → writing
- take → taking

### Short Verbs with a Consonant-Vowel-Consonant Pattern

The final consonant is often doubled.

- run → running
- sit → sitting
- swim → swimming

### Verbs Ending in -ie

Change **ie** to **y**.

- lie → lying
- die → dying

---

## Negative

Add **not** after **am, is, or are**.

**Subject + am/is/are + not + verb-ing**

- #### Examples:
  - I am not sleeping.
  - She is not working.
  - He isn't studying.
  - We aren't playing.
  - They aren't watching TV.
- #### Common contractions:
  - **is not → isn't**
  - **are not → aren't**
  - **I am not → I'm not**

---

## Questions

Put **am, is, or are** before the subject.

**Am/Is/Are + subject + verb-ing?**

- #### Examples:
  - Am I speaking too fast?
  - Are you studying?
  - Is he working?
  - Is she sleeping?
  - Are they playing football?

### Short Answers

- Are you studying? → **Yes, I am.**
- Is she working? → **Yes, she is.**
- Are they sleeping? → **No, they aren't.**

---

## Wh- Questions

Use a question word before **am, is, or are**.

**Wh-word + am/is/are + subject + verb-ing?**

- #### Examples:
  - What are you doing?
  - Where is she going?
  - What is he reading?
  - Why are they running?
  - Who is she talking to?

---

## Time Expressions

The Present Continuous is commonly used with expressions such as:

- now
- right now
- at the moment
- today
- this week
- this month

### Examples

- I am studying **now**.
- She is working **at the moment**.
- They are staying here **this week**.

---

## Present Simple vs. Present Continuous

The **Present Simple** is generally used for habits and routines.

The **Present Continuous** is generally used for actions happening now or around the present.

| Present Simple                   | Present Continuous             |
| -------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| I study every day.               | I am studying now.             |
| She works at a school.           | She is working now.            |
| They play football on Saturdays. | They are playing football now. |

Compare:

- I **read** every night.
  → A routine.

- I **am reading** a book now.
  → Happening now.

---

## Common Mistakes

### Forgetting the verb be

- ❌ I studying English.
- ✅ I am studying English.

### Using the base verb

- ❌ She is study English.
- ✅ She is studying English.

### Using do/does

- ❌ Does he working?
- ✅ Is he working?

### Forgetting -ing

- ❌ They are play football.
- ✅ They are playing football.

## Quick Summary

| Form         | Structure                            | Example            |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------ | ------------------ |
| Affirmative  | Subject + am/is/are + verb-ing       | She is working.    |
| Negative     | Subject + am/is/are + not + verb-ing | She isn't working. |
| Question     | Am/Is/Are + subject + verb-ing?      | Is she working?    |
| Wh- Question | Wh + am/is/are + subject + verb-ing? | What is she doing? |

### Be Forms

| Subject     | Be  |
| ----------- | --- |
| I           | am  |
| You/We/They | are |
| He/She/It   | is  |

**Present Continuous → actions happening now, temporary situations, and actions happening around the present.**
