We use **can** to talk about things that someone is able to do.

We use **can't** to say that someone is not able to do something.

---

## Affirmative

The basic structure is:

> **subject + can + base verb**

- I **can swim**.
- She **can speak** English.
- He **can drive**.
- We **can play** the guitar.
- They **can cook**.

The verb after **can** is always in the **base form**.

### Examples

- I **can run** fast.
- Maria **can dance**.
- My brother **can play** chess.
- We **can understand** English.

---

## Negative

Use **cannot** or **can't** to say that someone is not able to do something.

> **subject + can't + base verb**

- I **can't swim**.
- She **can't drive**.
- He **can't speak** French.
- We **can't play** tennis.

**Can't** is the common short form of **cannot**.

> I cannot swim.
> I can't swim.

---

## Can With Different Subjects

**Can** does not change depending on the subject.

| Subject | Example        |
| ------- | -------------- |
| I       | I can swim.    |
| You     | You can swim.  |
| He      | He can swim.   |
| She     | She can swim.  |
| It      | It can swim.   |
| We      | We can swim.   |
| They    | They can swim. |

There is no **-s** after _can_.

- ❌ She **cans** swim.
- ✅ She **can** swim.

---

## Can + Base Verb

Always use the base form of the verb after **can**.

- ❌ He can **swims**.
- ✅ He can **swim**.
- ❌ She can **speaks** English.
- ✅ She can **speak** English.
- ❌ I can **to swim**.
- ✅ I can **swim**.

### Structure

> **can + verb**

Not:

> **can + to + verb**

---

## Can for General Ability

We can use **can** to describe skills and abilities.

- I can read.
- She can write.
- He can cook.
- They can play football.
- We can speak Spanish.

We can also use **can't**:

- I can't sing.
- She can't drive.
- He can't swim.
- They can't speak Chinese.

---

## Common Mistakes

- ❌ She **cans** dance.
- ✅ She **can** dance.
- ❌ He can **to drive**.
- ✅ He can **drive**.
- ❌ I can **swims**.
- ✅ I can **swim**.
- ❌ She doesn't can swim.
- ✅ She **can't swim**.

---

## Quick Summary

- **Can** expresses ability.
- **Can't** expresses inability.
- Use **can + base verb**.
- **Can** does not change with the subject.
- Do not use **to** after _can_.
- Do not add **-s** to the verb after _can_.
