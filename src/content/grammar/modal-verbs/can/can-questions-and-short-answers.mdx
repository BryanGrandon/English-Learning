We use **can** at the beginning of a question.

Unlike questions with **do** or **does**, we do not use an auxiliary verb with _can_.

---

## Yes / No Questions

The basic structure is:

> **Can + subject + base verb?**

- #### Examples:
  - **Can you swim?**
  - **Can she drive?**
  - **Can he speak English?**
  - **Can they play football?**
  - **Can we come with you?**

---

## Short Answers

Use **can** or **can't** in short answers.

### Positive

> **Yes, + subject + can.**

- Can you swim?
  - **Yes, I can.**
- Can she drive?
  - **Yes, she can.**
- Can they speak English?
  - **Yes, they can.**

### Negative

> **No, + subject + can't.**

- Can you swim?
  - **No, I can't.**
- Can he drive?
  - **No, he can't.**
- Can they come?
  - **No, they can't.**

---

## Question Structure

Compare the structure with other verbs.

### With Can

> **Can + subject + base verb?**

- Can you swim?
- Can she cook?

### With Other Verbs

> **Do/Does + subject + base verb?**

- Do you swim?
- Does she cook?

**Can** moves directly to the beginning of the question.

---

## Can With Different Subjects

| Subject | Question       | Short Answer    |
| ------- | -------------- | --------------- |
| I       | Can I go?      | Yes, you can.   |
| you     | Can you swim?  | Yes, I can.     |
| he      | Can he drive?  | No, he can't.   |
| she     | Can she cook?  | Yes, she can.   |
| it      | Can it move?   | No, it can't.   |
| we      | Can we enter?  | Yes, you can.   |
| they    | Can they help? | No, they can't. |

---

## No Do / Does

Do not use **do** or **does** with _can_.

- ❌ **Do you can swim?**
- ✅ **Can you swim?**
- ❌ **Does she can drive?**
- ✅ **Can she drive?**

---

## No To After Can

Do not use **to** after _can_.

- ❌ Can you **to help** me?
- ✅ Can you **help** me?
- ❌ Can she **to swim**?
- ✅ Can she **swim**?

---

## Common Mistakes

- ❌ Can he **swims**?
- ✅ Can he **swim**?
- ❌ Does she **can** cook?
- ✅ **Can she** cook?
- ❌ Yes, she **can to**.
- ✅ Yes, she **can**.
- ❌ No, he **doesn't can**.
- ✅ No, he **can't**.

---

## Quick Summary

- Put **can** before the subject in questions.
- Use **can + subject + base verb?**
- Use **Yes, subject + can** for positive answers.
- Use **No, subject + can't** for negative answers.
- Do not use **do/does** with _can_.
- Do not use **to** after _can_.
