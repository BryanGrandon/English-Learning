Nouns can be singular or plural.

- A singular noun refers to one person, place, animal, thing, or idea.
- A plural noun refers to two or more people, places, animals, things, or ideas.

---

## Singular Nouns

We use a singular noun when we talk about one person, place, animal, thing, or idea.

| Category | Singular |
| -------- | -------- |
| Person   | student  |
| Place    | city     |
| Animal   | dog      |
| Thing    | book     |
| Idea     | idea     |

- #### Examples
  - I have a book.
  - She is a student.
  - This is a city.
  - The dog is outside.

---

## Plural Nouns

We use a plural noun when we talk about two or more people, places, animals, things, or ideas.

| Category | Plural   |
| -------- | -------- |
| Person   | students |
| Place    | cities   |
| Animal   | dogs     |
| Thing    | books    |
| Idea     | ideas    |

- #### Examples
  - I have three books.
  - They are students.
  - These are cities.
  - The dogs are outside.

---

## Singular vs. Plural

Compare the singular and plural forms:

| Singular | Plural   |
| -------- | -------- |
| book     | books    |
| student  | students |
| dog      | dogs     |
| city     | cities   |
| child    | children |

The plural form is often different from the singular form. The way a noun changes depends on the noun.

- #### For example:
  - book -> books
  - city -> cities
  - child -> children

The specific rules for forming plural nouns are covered in Regular Plurals and Irregular Plural Nouns.

---

## Singular and Plural with Articles

Singular countable nouns can be used with a/an.

- a book
- a car
- an apple
- an orange

We do not use a/an with plural nouns.

- books
- cars
- apples
- oranges

### Compare

- I have a book.
- I have three books.
- She is a teacher.
- They are teachers.

---

## Singular and Plural with Be

The number of the noun also affects the form of the verb be.

### Singular

Use is with singular nouns.

- The boy is happy.
- The dog is outside.
- The book is interesting.

### Plural

Use are with plural nouns.

- The boys are happy.
- The dogs are outside.
- The books are interesting.

### Compare

| Singular                 | Plural                     |
| ------------------------ | -------------------------- |
| The dog is outside.      | The dogs are outside.      |
| The book is interesting. | The books are interesting. |
| The student is here.     | The students are here.     |
