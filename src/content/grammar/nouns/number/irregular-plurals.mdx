Most plural nouns are formed by adding -s or -es. However, some nouns do not follow these regular patterns.

These nouns are called irregular plural nouns.

Their plural forms may change completely, change only part of the word, or stay the same.

---

## What Are Irregular Plurals?

An irregular plural is a plural noun that does not follow the usual rules for forming regular plurals.

### Compare:

| Regular         | Irregular         |
| --------------- | ----------------- |
| book -> books   | child -> children |
| car -> cars     | man -> men        |
| bus -> buses    | person -> people  |
| apple -> apples | tooth -> teeth    |

Regular nouns usually follow a predictable pattern, while irregular nouns have to be learned individually.

---

## Common Irregular Plurals

Here are some common irregular plural nouns:

| Singular | Plural   |
| -------- | -------- |
| man      | men      |
| woman    | women    |
| child    | children |
| person   | people   |
| tooth    | teeth    |
| foot     | feet     |
| mouse    | mice     |
| goose    | geese    |

### Examples

- One man is waiting outside.
- Two men are waiting outside.
- The child is playing.
- The children are playing.
- I have a toothache.
- My teeth are clean.

---

## Plurals That Change the Vowel

Some irregular nouns change their internal vowel when they become plural.

| Singular | Plural |
| -------- | ------ |
| man      | men    |
| woman    | women  |
| foot     | feet   |
| tooth    | teeth  |
| mouse    | mice   |
| goose    | geese  |

### Examples

- One man -> two men
- One foot -> two feet
- One tooth -> two teeth
- One mouse -> two mice

These forms cannot be created by simply adding -s or -es.

---

## Plurals That Change Completely

Some nouns have a completely different plural form.

| Singular | Plural   |
| -------- | -------- |
| person   | people   |
| child    | children |

### Examples

- One person is in the room.
- Three people are in the room.
- The child is happy.
- The children are happy.

---

## Plurals That Stay the Same

Some nouns have the same form in the singular and plural.

| Singular | Plural |
| -------- | ------ |
| sheep    | sheep  |
| fish     | fish   |
| deer     | deer   |

The meaning depends on the context or on words such as one, two, or many.

### Examples

- I see one sheep.
- I see three sheep.
- There is a deer in the forest.
- There are two deer in the forest.

Some nouns can have alternative plural forms depending on meaning or variety of English. For example, fish can also have the plural fishes in specific contexts.

---

## Irregular Plurals with Articles

Use a/an with a singular countable noun.

- a man
- a woman
- a child
- a person
- a mouse

Do not use a/an with plural nouns.

- men
- women
- children
- people
- mice

### Compare

- There is a child in the park.
- There are three children in the park.
- I see a mouse.
- I see two mice.

---

## Irregular Plurals with Be

Remember that plural nouns generally use are with the verb be.

### Singular

- The man is here.
- The child is happy.
- The mouse is small.

### Plural

- The men are here.
- The children are happy.
- The mice are small.

### Compare

| Singular            | Plural                  |
| ------------------- | ----------------------- |
| The man is here.    | The men are here.       |
| The child is happy. | The children are happy. |
| The mouse is small. | The mice are small.     |

---

## Common Mistakes

Do not add -s or -es to irregular plural nouns.

- ❌ two childs
- ✅ two children
- ❌ three mans
- ✅ three men
- ❌ two mouses
- ✅ two mice
- ❌ five persons
- ✅ five people (in general contexts)
