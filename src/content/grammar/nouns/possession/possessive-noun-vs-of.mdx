Both possessive 's and of + noun can show possession or a relationship between two nouns.

However, they are used differently depending on the type of noun and the context.

---

## Possessive 's

We commonly use 's with people, animals, and groups.

**_possessor + 's + noun_**

- Sarah's book
- the dog's tail
- my parents' house
- the company's website

This is Daniel's computer.

---

## Of-Possessive

We commonly use of + noun with things, places, and concepts.

**_the + noun + of + noun_**

- the door of the house
- the name of the city
- the color of the car
- the history of the country

What is the name of the city?

---

## Compare the Two Forms

| Possessive 's         | Of-Possessive              |
| --------------------- | -------------------------- |
| Maria's book          | the cover of the book      |
| the dog's tail        | the door of the house      |
| the teacher's desk    | the name of the school     |
| the company's website | the history of the company |
| Chile's capital       | the capital of Chile       |

The two structures are not always interchangeable.

### People

With people, 's is usually more natural.

- John's car is new.

The form:

- the car of John

is grammatically possible in some contexts, but it is much less natural in everyday English.

### Things

With things, of is often natural.

- the roof of the house
- the door of the car
- the end of the movie

We can sometimes use 's with things, especially when the thing is associated with time, place, organizations, or institutions.

- the company's office
- the city's center
- today's news
- the world's population

### Animals

Both forms can be possible with animals.

- the dog's tail
- the tail of the dog

The 's form is usually more natural in everyday English.

- The cat's eyes are green.

---

## Common Mistake

Do not automatically use of when talking about people.

- ❌ the book of Sarah
- ✅ Sarah's book

Also avoid forcing 's with every noun.

- ❌ the house's door
- ✅ the door of the house

---

## A Useful Rule

As a general guide:

- #### People / animals / groups → 's
  - Anna's bag
  - the dog's food
  - the company's employees
- #### Things / places / concepts → of
  - the door of the house
  - the name of the city
  - the end of the story

There are exceptions, so this is a useful guideline rather than an absolute rule.

---

## Quick Summary

| Use      | Common Structure | Example                 |
| -------- | ---------------- | ----------------------- |
| People   | 's               | Anna's book             |
| Animals  | 's               | the dog's tail          |
| Groups   | 's               | the team's coach        |
| Things   | of               | the door of the house   |
| Places   | of               | the center of the city  |
| Concepts | of               | the meaning of the word |

The choice between 's and of depends mainly on the type of noun and what sounds natural in context.
