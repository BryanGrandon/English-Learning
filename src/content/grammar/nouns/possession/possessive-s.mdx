---
title: Possessive -s
translation: Verbos Posesivos
level: A2
topic: nouns
theme: possession
order: 4
---

We use 's to show possession, belonging, or relationships.

The basic structure is:

- #### **_possessor + 's + thing/person_**
  - Emma's book
  - The book belongs to Emma.

---

## Singular Nouns

Add 's to a singular noun.

- the boy's bicycle
- the girl's bag
  -the teacher's desk
- Michael's phone
- the dog's tail

### Examples

- This is Sarah's room.
- That is the dog's toy.
- My brother's car is red.

---

## Singular Names Ending in -s

For names ending in -s, 's is commonly used.

- James's car
- Chris's phone
- Lucas's bike

James's house is near the school.

---

## Plural Nouns Ending in -s

For regular plural nouns ending in -s, add only an apostrophe.

- #### plural noun + '
  - the students' books
  - the girls' bags
  - the teachers' room
  - my parents' house

### Compare:

| Singular             | Plural               |
| -------------------- | -------------------- |
| the girl's bag       | the girls' bags      |
| the student's book   | the students' books  |
| the teacher's office | the teachers' office |

---

## Irregular Plural Nouns

Irregular plural nouns do not end in -s, so we add 's.

- children's toys
- men's clothes
- women's shoes
- people's opinions

The children's toys are on the floor.

---

## Possessive 's with Names

We can use 's with people's names.

- Daniel's computer
- Sofia's notebook
- Laura's brother
- Tom's bicycle

This is Daniel's notebook.

---

## Common Mistakes

Do not confuse 's with the plural form.

- ❌ two student's
- ✅ two students
- ❌ three dog's
- ✅ three dogs

The apostrophe shows possession, not a plural.

---

## Quick Summary

| Noun     | Possessive |
| -------- | ---------- |
| girl     | girl's     |
| student  | student's  |
| girls    | girls'     |
| students | students'  |
| child    | child's    |
| children | children's |

Use 's with singular nouns and irregular plurals. Use ' with regular plurals ending in -s.
