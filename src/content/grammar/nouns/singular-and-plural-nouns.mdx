Nouns can be singular or plural, depending on the number of people, places, animals, things, or ideas they refer to.

- **Singular nouns** -> Refer to one person or thing.
- **Plural nouns** -> Refer to two or more people or things.

**_Los sustantivos pueden estar en singular o plural, dependiendo de la cantidad de personas, lugares, animales, cosas o ideas a las que se refieren._**

---

## Singular Nouns

A singular noun refers to one person, place, animal, thing, or idea.

We use singular nouns when we talk about just one.

**_Un sustantivo singular se refiere a una sola persona, lugar, animal, cosa o idea._**

<div className='table-wrapper'>

| Category | Singular |
| -------- | -------- |
| Person   | student  |
| Place    | city     |
| Animal   | dog      |
| Thing    | book     |
| Idea     | idea     |

</div>

- #### Examples in Sentences
  - I have a book. **_(Tengo un libro)_**.
  - She is a student. **_(Ella es estudiante)_**.
  - This is a city. **_(Esta es una ciudad)_**.
  - The dog is outside. **_(El perro está afuera)_**.
  - Happiness is important. **_(La felicidad es importante)_**.

The highlighted words are singular nouns because they refer to one person or thing.

**_Las palabras destacadas son sustantivos singulares porque se refieren a una sola persona o cosa._**

---

## Plural Nouns

A plural noun refers to two or more people, places, animals, things, or ideas.

We use plural nouns when we talk about more than one.

**_Un sustantivo plural se refiere a dos o más personas, lugares, animales, cosas o ideas._**

<div className='table-wrapper'>

| Category | Plural   |
| -------- | -------- |
| Person   | students |
| Place    | cities   |
| Animal   | dogs     |
| Thing    | books    |
| Idea     | ideas    |

</div>

- #### Examples in Sentences
  - I have three books. **_(Tengo tres libros)_**.
  - They are students. **_(Ellos son estudiantes)_**.
  - These are cities. **_(Estas son ciudades)_**.
  - The dogs are outside. **_(Los perros están afuera)_**.
  - Ideas are important. **_(Las ideas son importantes)_**.

The highlighted words are plural nouns because they refer to more than one person or thing.

**_Las palabras destacadas son sustantivos plurales porque se refieren a más de una persona o cosa._**

---

## Singular vs. Plural

A noun can change its form depending on whether it refers to one or more than one.

<div className='table-wrapper'>

| Singular | Plural   |
| -------- | -------- |
| book     | books    |
| student  | students |
| dog      | dogs     |
| city     | cities   |
| child    | children |
| person   | people   |

</div>

#### Examples in Sentences

- This is a book. **_(Este es un libro)_**.
- These are books. **_(Estos son libros)_**.
- The child is happy. **_(El niño está feliz)_**.
- The children are happy. **_(Los niños están felices)_**.

Notice that some nouns simply add a letter or letters, while others change their form completely.

**_Observa que algunos sustantivos simplemente agregan una o varias letras, mientras que otros cambian completamente su forma._**

---

## How to Form Plural Nouns

There are different rules for forming plural nouns.

- Regular Plurals → Usually add -s or -es.
- Irregular Plurals → Change their form in different ways.

- #### Examples:
  - book -> books
  - box -> boxes
  - child -> children

You will learn these rules in the lessons Regular Plural Nouns and Irregular Plural Nouns.

## Singular and Plural with Articles

Singular countable nouns can use the articles a or an.

- a book
- a car
- an apple
- an orange

We do not use a or an with plural nouns.

- books
- cars
- apples
- oranges

- #### Compare These Examples
  - I have a book. **_(Tengo un libro)_**.
  - I have three books. **_(Tengo tres libros)_**.
  - She is a teacher. **_(Ella es profesora)_**.
  - They are teachers. **_(Ellos son profesores)_**.

**_Los artículos a y an se utilizan con sustantivos contables en singular, pero no con sustantivos en plural._**

---

Singular and Plural with This, That, These, and Those

We also use different demonstrative words depending on whether a noun is singular or plural.

<div className='table-wrapper'>

| Singular   | Plural       |
| ---------- | ------------ |
| this book  | these books  |
| that dog   | those dogs   |
| this car   | these cars   |
| that house | those houses |

</div>

- This / That → Used with singular nouns.
- These / Those → Used with plural nouns.

- #### Examples
  - This book is interesting. **_(Este libro es interesante)_**.
  - These books are interesting. **_(Estos libros son interesantes)_**.
  - That dog is big. **_(Ese perro es grande)_**.
  - Those dogs are big. **_(Esos perros son grandes)_**.

**_This y that se utilizan con sustantivos singulares, mientras que these y those se utilizan con sustantivos plurales._**

---

## Singular and Plural with the Verb Be

The number of a noun also affects the form of the verb be.

We use is with singular subjects and are with plural subjects.

**_El número del sujeto también afecta la forma del verbo be. Utilizamos is con sujetos singulares y are con sujetos plurales._**

### Singular

Use is with singular nouns.

The boy is happy. **_(El niño está feliz)_**.
The dog is outside. **_(El perro está afuera)_**.
The book is interesting. **_(El libro es interesante)_**.

### Plural

Use are with plural nouns.

- The boys are happy. **_(Los niños están felices)_**.
- The dogs are outside. **_(Los perros están afuera)_**.
- The books are interesting. **_(Los libros son interesantes)_**.

### Compare These Examples

| Singular                 | Plural                     |
| ------------------------ | -------------------------- |
| The dog is outside.      | The dogs are outside.      |
| The book is interesting. | The books are interesting. |
| The student is here.     | The students are here.     |

- #### Remember:
  - Singular → is
  - Plural → are

## Summary

Nouns can be singular or plural depending on the number of people, places, animals, things, or ideas they refer to.

- **Singular Noun**: Refers to one person or thing.
- **Plural Noun**: Refers to two or more people or things.
