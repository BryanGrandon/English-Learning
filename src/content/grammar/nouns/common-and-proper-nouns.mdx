Nouns can name people, places, things, animals, and ideas in two different ways: generally or specifically.

There are two main types of nouns:

- Common nouns -> General names.
- Proper nouns -> Specific names.

**_Los sustantivos pueden nombrar personas, lugares, cosas, animales e ideas de dos maneras: de forma general o específica._**

---

## Common Nouns

A common noun is a general name for a person, place, thing, animal, or idea. It does not identify a specific one.

Common nouns are usually written with a lowercase letter, unless they appear at the beginning of a sentence.

**_Un sustantivo común es un nombre general para una persona, lugar, cosa, animal o idea. No identifica a uno en particular._**

- #### Example
  - I have a book. **_(Tengo un libro)_**.
  - She is a teacher. **_(Ella es profesora)_**.
  - We live in a city. **_(Vivimos en una ciudad)_**.
  - The dog is sleeping. **_(El perro está durmiendo)_**.
  - Music makes me happy. **_(La música me hace feliz)_**.

The highlighted words are common nouns because they name people, places, things, animals, or ideas in a general way.

**_Las palabras destacadas son sustantivos comunes porque nombran personas, lugares, cosas, animales o ideas de manera general._**

---

## Proper Nouns

A proper noun is the specific name of a person, place, organization, day, month, holiday, or other particular thing.

Proper nouns always begin with a **capital letter**, no matter where they appear in a sentence.

**_Un sustantivo propio es el nombre específico de una persona, lugar, organización, día, mes, festividad u otra cosa en particular._**

- #### Examples
  - My name is Carlos. **_(Mi nombre es Carlos)_**.
  - He works in New York. **_(Él trabaja en Nueva York)_**.
  - They are from Chile. **_(Ellos son de Chile)_**.
  - We have English class on Monday. **_(Tenemos clases de inglés el lunes)_**.
  - My birthday is in January. **_(Mi cumpleaños es en enero)_**.
  - I study at Harvard University. **_(Estudio en la Universidad de Harvard)_**.

The highlighted words are proper nouns because they identify specific people, places, organizations, or dates.

**_Las palabras destacadas son sustantivos propios porque identifican personas, lugares, organizaciones o fechas específicas._**

---

## Common vs. Proper Nouns

The main difference between common and proper nouns is whether they refer to something general or specific.

**_La principal diferencia entre los sustantivos comunes y propios es si se refieren a algo general o específico._**

### Compare These Examples

| Common Noun | Proper Noun        |
| ----------- | ------------------ |

- #### Example in a Sentence
  - I live in a city.
  - I live in Santiago.

In the first sentence, city is a common noun because it refers to a place in general.

In the second sentence, Santiago is a proper noun because it identifies a specific city.

**_En la primera oración, city es un sustantivo común porque se refiere a un lugar de manera general. En la segunda, Santiago es un sustantivo propio porque identifica una ciudad específica._**

- #### More Examples
  - My **friend** is called **Sofia**.
  - I study at a **school** in **Chile**.
  - My **dog** is named **Max**.

Notice how common and proper nouns can appear together in the same sentence.

**_Observa cómo los sustantivos comunes y propios pueden aparecer juntos en una misma oración._**

---

## Capitalization Rules

One important difference between common and proper nouns is how we write them.

### Common Nouns

Common nouns usually begin with a lowercase letter.

- book
- teacher
- city
- dog

However, they begin with a capital letter when they appear at the beginning of a sentence.

- #### Example
  - The dog is sleeping.

### Proper Nouns

Proper nouns always begin with a capital letter.

- Carlos
- London
- Chile
- Monday
- January

Remember that days of the week and months are always capitalized in English.

**_Recuerda que los días de la semana y los meses siempre se escriben con mayúscula inicial en inglés._**

---

## Summary

Common and proper nouns are two types of nouns that help us identify people, places, things, animals, and ideas.

- **Common Noun**: A general name.
- **Proper Noun**: A specific name.
- **Capitalization**: Common nouns usually begin with lowercase letters, while proper nouns always begin with capital letters.
