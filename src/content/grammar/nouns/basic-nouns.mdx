Nouns are one of the most important parts of English. They are used to name people, places, things, animals, and ideas.

In this lesson, you will learn what nouns are and how they work in sentences, including their roles as subjects and objects.

- #### People
  - teacher
  - student
  - doctor
  - Maria
- #### Places
  - school
  - city
  - park
  - Chile
- #### Things
  - book
  - computer
  - table
  - phone
- #### Animals
  - dog
  - cat
  - bird
  - horse
- #### Ideas
  - love
  - happiness
  - freedom
  - knowledge

### Examples

- **Maria** is a teacher.
- I have a **book**.
- The **dod** is sleeping.
- **Happiness** is important

The words Maria, teacher, book, dog, and happiness are nouns.

---

## Nouns in a Sentence

Nouns can appear in different positions in a sentence. Their position and function depend on how they are being used.

- #### Compare these examples:
  - **John** reads a book.
  - John reads a **book**.

In the first sentence, **John** is the person performing the action. It is the subject.

In the second sentence, **book** receives the action. It is the object.

A noun can therefore have different roles depending on its position in the sentence.

---

## Nouns as Subjects

The subject is the person, place, thing, animal, or idea that performs an action or is being described.

- #### Structure
  - Subject + Verb + ...
- #### Example
  - **Sarah** works at a school.
  - The **dog** runs in the park.
  - My **brother** plays football.
  - The **computer** is new.

The nouns Sarah, dog, brother, and computer are subjects.

### Finding the Subject

To identify the subject, ask: _Who or what performs the action?_

- Example: The boy opens the door.
  - Action: _Who opens the door?_
  - Subject: **boy**
- Example:The cat sleeps on the sofa.
  - Action: _Who sleeps?_
  - Subject: **cat**

---

## Nouns as Objects

An object is a person, place, thing, animal, or idea that receives the action of the verb.

- #### Structure
  - Subject + Verb + Object
- #### Examples
  - Sarah reads a **book**.
  - The dog eats the **food**.
  - John opened the **door**.
  - Maria likes **music**.

  The nouns book, food, door, and music are objects.

### Finding the Object

To identify the object, ask: _Who or what receives the action?_

- Example: The boy opens the door.
  - Action: _What does the boy open?_
  - object: **The door**
- Example: Sarah reads a book.
  - Action: _What does Sarah read?_
  - object: _A book_

---

## Subject vs. Object

A noun can be a subject in one sentence and an object in another.

- #### Compare
  - The **dog** sees the cat.
  - The cat sees the **dog**.

In the first sentence, dog is the subject because the dog performs the action.

In the second sentence, dog is the object because the dog receives the action.

- Subject → Performs the action
- Object → Receives the action

---

## Summary

A noun names a person, place, thing, animal, or idea.

Nouns can have different roles in a sentence:

- Subject → performs the action or is being described.
- Object → receives the action.
