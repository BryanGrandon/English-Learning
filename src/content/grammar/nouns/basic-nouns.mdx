## Introduction

Nouns are words used to name people, places, things, animals, and ideas. They are one of the most important parts of English because they help us talk about the world around us.

**_Los sustantivos son palabras que utilizamos para nombrar personas, lugares, cosas, animales e ideas. Son una de las partes más importantes del inglés porque nos permiten hablar sobre el mundo que nos rodea._**

---

## Types of Nouns

Nouns can name different things:

The highlighted words are nouns because they name people, things, animals, or ideas.

Las palabras destacadas son sustantivos porque nombran personas, cosas, animales o ideas.

---

## How to identify a Noun

A noun names a person, place, thing, animal, or idea. You can often identify nouns by asking these questions:

- **Who?** → teacher, doctor, Maria.
- **Where?** → school, park, Chile.
- **What?** → book, computer, happiness.

- #### Examples
  - My **teacher** is nice. **_(Mi profesor es amable)_**.
  - I live in **Chile**. **(Vivo en Chile)**.
  - She has a **computer**. **_(Ella tiene un computador)_**.

Remember that nouns can appear in different parts of a sentence and have different grammatical roles.

**_Recuerda que los sustantivos pueden aparecer en diferentes partes de una oración y cumplir distintas funciones gramaticales_**

---

## Nouns in a Sentence

Nouns can have different roles in a sentence. Two common roles are subjects and objects.

**_Los sustantivos pueden cumplir diferentes funciones dentro de una oración. Dos funciones comunes son el sujeto y el objeto._**

- #### Compare these examples:
  - **John** reads a book.
  - John reads a **book**.

In the first sentence, **John** is the person performing the action. It is the subject.

In the second sentence, **book** receives the action. It is the object.

A noun can therefore have different roles depending on its position in the sentence.

---

## Nouns as Subjects

The subject is the person, place, thing, or idea that a sentence is about. It often performs the action of the verb.

**_El sujeto es la persona, lugar, cosa o idea de la que se habla en una oración. Generalmente realiza la acción del verbo._**

- #### Structure
  - **_Subject + Verb + ..._**
- #### Example
  - **Sarah** works at a school. **_(Sarah trabaja en una escuela)_**.
  - The **dog** runs in the park. **_(El perro corre en el parque)_**.
  - My **brother** plays football. **_(Mi hermano juega fútbol)_**.
  - The **computer** is new. **_(El computador es nuevo)_**.
  - **Sarah** is happy. **_(Sarah está feliz)_**.

The highlighted nouns are subjects because they are what the sentences are about.

### Finding the Subject

- To identify the subject, ask:
  - **Who or what is the sentence about?**
  - **_Para identificar el sujeto, pregúntate: ¿De quién o de qué se habla en la oración?_**
- You can also ask:
  - **Who or what performs the action?**
  - **_¿Quién o qué realiza la acción?_**

### Examples

- #### The boy opens the door.
  - Action: opens.
  - Question: Who opens the door?
  - Subject: The boy.

- #### The cat sleeps on the sofa.
  - Action: sleeps.
  - Question: Who sleeps on the sofa?
  - Subject: The cat.

- #### The computer is new.
  - Description: is new.
  - Question: What is the sentence about?
  - Subject: The computer.

## Nouns as Objects

direct object is the person or thing that receives the action of the verb.

**_El objeto directo es la persona o cosa que recibe la acción del verbo._**

- #### Structure
  - **Subject + Verb + Object**
- #### Examples
  - Sarah reads a book. **_(Sarah lee un libro)_**.
  - The dog eats the food. **_(El perro come la comida)_**.
  - John opened the door. **_(John abrió la puerta)_**.
  - Maria likes music. **_(A María le gusta la música)_**.

The highlighted nouns are direct objects because they receive the action of the verb.

### Finding the Object

- To identify the direct object, ask:
  - **Who or what receives the action?**
  - **_Para identificar el objeto directo, pregúntate: ¿Quién o qué recibe la acción?_**

### Examples

- #### The boy opens the door.
  - Action: opens.
  - Question: What does the boy open?
  - Object: The door.

- #### Sarah reads a book.
  - Action: reads.
  - Question: What does Sarah read?
  - Object: A book.

---

## Subject vs. Object

The same noun can be a subject in one sentence and an object in another. Its role depends on how it is used.

**_Un mismo sustantivo puede ser sujeto en una oración y objeto en otra. Su función depende de cómo se utiliza._**

- #### Compare These Examples
  - The dog sees the cat.
  - The cat sees the dog.

In the first sentence, dog is the subject because it performs the action.

**_En la primera oración, dog es el sujeto porque realiza la acción._**

In the second sentence, dog is the object because it receives the action.

**_En la segunda oración, dog es el objeto porque recibe la acción._**

### Remember

- **Subject**: The person or thing the sentence is about.
- **Direct Object**: The person or thing that receives the action.

**_Recuerda: el sujeto es aquello de lo que se habla en la oración, mientras que el objeto directo recibe la acción del verbo._**

---

## Summary

A noun is a word that names a person, place, thing, animal, or idea.

**_Un sustantivo es una palabra que nombra una persona, lugar, cosa, animal o idea._**

Nouns can have different roles in a sentence:

- **Subject**: The person or thing the sentence is about.
- **Direct Object**: The person or thing that receives the action.
