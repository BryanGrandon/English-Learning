Nouns can name something in a general way or identify a specific person, place, thing, or organization.

- #### There are two important types of nouns:
  - Common nouns - general names
  - Proper nouns - specific names

---

## Common Nouns

A common noun is a _general name_ for a person, place, thing, animal, or idea.

Common nouns are usually written with a _lowercase letter_, unless they appear at the beginning of a sentence.

- #### Example
  - I have a **book**.
  - She is a **teacher**.
  - We live in a **city**.
  - The **dog** is sleeping.

The words **book**, **teacher**, **city**, and **dog** are _common nouns_ because they describe things in a general way.

---

## Proper Nouns

A proper noun is the _specific name_ of a person, place, organization, day, month, or other particular thing.

Proper nouns always begin with a _capital letter_.

- #### Examples
  - My name is **Carlos**.
  - She lives in **London**.
  - They are from **Chile**.
  - We have English class on **Monday**.

**Carlos**, **London**, **Chile**, and **Monday** are _proper nouns_ because they identify specific people, places, countries, or days.

---

## Common vs. Proper Nouns

The main difference is whether the noun refers to something general or specific.

<div>

| Common Noun | Proper Noun        |
| ----------- | ------------------ |
| city        | London             |
| country     | Chile              |
| person      | Maria              |
| school      | Harvard University |
| month       | January            |
| day         | Monday             |
| animal      | Max                |
| restaurant  | McDonald's         |

</div>

- #### Compare
  - I live in a **city**.
  - I live in **Santiago**.

1. **City** is a _common noun_ because it is a general name.
2. **Santiago** is a _proper noun_ because it is the specific name of a city.

---

## Common Mistake

### Capital Letters

Proper nouns begin with a capital letter.

- Common noun → usually lowercase
- Proper noun → capital letter

---

## Quick Comparison

<div>

| Category     | Common Nouns | Proper Nouns |
| ------------ | ------------ | ------------ |
| Person       | girl         | Sofia        |
| Place        | country      | Chile        |
| Thing        | day          | Monday       |
| Animal       | dog          | Max          |
| Organization | company      | Google       |

</div>

The _common noun_ gives a **_general name_**, while the _proper noun_ gives a **_specific name_**.

---

## Summary

- A common noun is a general name.
- A proper noun is a specific name.
- Common nouns are usually written in lowercase.
- Proper nouns always begin with a capital letter.
- A noun can be common or proper depending on what it refers to.

### Remember

- #### General → Common Noun
  - city, country, teacher, dog
- #### Specific → Proper Noun
  - London, Chile, Mr. Smith, Max
