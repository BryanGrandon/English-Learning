Adjectives are words that describe nouns. They give us more information about a person, place, animal, thing, or idea.

---

## What Is an Adjective?

An adjective is a word that describes a noun.

Adjectives can tell us about:

- size: big, small
- quality: good, bad
- age: new, old
- color: red, blue
- appearance: beautiful, clean

### Examples

- a big house
- a small dog
- a good student
- an old car
- a new phone

---

## Adjectives Describe Nouns

Adjectives give us more information about nouns.

- #### Compare
  - I have a car.
  - I have a new car.

The word new describes the noun car.

- #### Another example
  - She has a dog.
  - She has a small dog.

The word small describes the noun dog.

### More Examples

| Adjective | Noun    | Phrase         |
| --------- | ------- | -------------- |
| big       | house   | a big house    |
| small     | dog     | a small dog    |
| good      | student | a good student |
| bad       | idea    | a bad idea     |
| new       | phone   | a new phone    |
| old       | car     | an old car     |

## Adjectives Do Not Change for Singular or Plural Nouns

In English, adjectives normally do not change when the noun is singular or plural.

The adjective stays the same.

- #### Singular
  - a big house
  - a small dog
- #### Plural
  - big houses
  - small dogs

Notice that big and small do not change.

- ❌ bigs houses
- ❌ smalls dogs
- ✅ big houses
- ✅ small dogs

---

## Adjectives With Singular and Plural Nouns

| Singular     | Plural     |
| ------------ | ---------- |
| a new car    | new cars   |
| an old house | old houses |
| a good book  | good books |
| a small dog  | small dogs |
| a big apple  | big apples |

The noun changes from singular to plural, but the adjective does not.

---

## Adjectives in Sentences

Adjectives can be used to describe nouns in simple sentences.

- This is a big house.
- I have a new phone.
- She has a small dog.
- He drives an old car.
- They have a good teacher.
- We live in a small apartment.
- These are new books.
- Those are old buildings.

---

## Quick Summary

- An adjective describes a noun.
- Adjectives give us more information about nouns.
- Common adjectives include big, small, good, bad, new, and old.
- Adjectives normally do not change for singular or plural nouns.
