Personal pronouns are words we use instead of nouns to refer to people, animals, or things. They can have different forms depending on their function in a sentence.

In English, personal pronouns can change depending on their function in the sentence. The two main types are subject pronouns and object pronouns.

<ContentGrid>
<LessonBlock type='structure' title='Subject Pronouns' fullWidth>

Subject + Verb

- **_I_** am a student.
- **_You_** speak English.
- **_He_** works here.
- **_She_** is my sister.
- **_It_** is cold.
- **_We_** live in Chile.
- **_They_** play soccer.

</LessonBlock>
<LessonBlock type='structure' title='Object Pronouns' fullWidth>

Verb + Object

- She called **_me_**.
- I saw **_you_**.
- We helped **_him_**.
- They invited **_her_**.
- I like **_it_**.
- She told **_us_**.
- He knows **_them_**.

</LessonBlock> 
</ContentGrid>

## Subject and Object Pronouns

Subject and object pronouns refer to the same people or things, but they have different functions in a sentence.

<ContentGrid columns={3}>

<LessonBlock type='comparison' title='Subject vs. Object' fullWidth>

| Subject | Object |
| ------- | ------ |
| I       | me     |
| you     | you    |
| he      | him    |
| she     | her    |
| it      | it     |
| we      | us     |
| they    | them   |

</LessonBlock>

<LessonBlock type='common_mistake' title='Subject or Object?' fullWidth className='col-span-2' > 
Do not use an object pronoun as the subject of a sentence.

- ❌ Him is my friend.
- ✅ He is my friend.

Do not use a subject pronoun as the object of a verb.

- ❌ She called I.

</LessonBlock>
</ContentGrid>

## Subject Pronouns

Subject pronouns are used as the subject of a sentence. They tell us who or what performs the action.

<LessonBlock type='rule' fullWidth >

Use a **subject pronoun** when the pronoun performs the action of the verb.

- She reads every day.
- They live in Chile.

</LessonBlock>

---

## Object Pronouns

Object pronouns are used as the object of a verb or after a preposition. They receive the action or are affected by it.

<LessonBlock type='rule' fullWidth >

Use an **object pronoun** when the pronoun receives the action of a verb or follows a preposition.

- She called me.
- I like him.
- They invited us.

</LessonBlock>

---

## The Pronoun It

The pronoun **it** is used to refer to a **thing, animal, place, situation, or idea**. It can be used as both a subject pronoun and an object pronoun.

<ContentGrid>
<LessonBlock type='structure' title='It as a Subject Pronoun' >

Use **it** as the subject when it refers to a thing, animal, place, situation, or idea.

It + Verb

- **It** is my phone.
- **It** is very cold today.
- **It** looks interesting.

</LessonBlock>
<LessonBlock type='structure' title='It as an Object Pronoun' >

Use **it** as the object when it receives the action of a verb or follows a preposition.

Verb + it

- I like **it**.
- She bought **it**.
- We need **it**.

</LessonBlock>
</ContentGrid>
