Common nouns are words we use to name people, animals, places, objects, and things in general.
They do not refer to one specific person, place, or thing.

---

## Common Nouns vs. Proper Nouns

The main difference is that common nouns refer to general names, while proper nouns refer to specific names.

<ContentGrid> 
<LessonBlock type='comparison' title='Common vs. Proper Nouns' fullWidth>

| Common Noun | Proper Noun       |
| ----------- | ----------------- |
| city        | London            |
| country     | Chile             |
| teacher     | Mr. Brown         |
| dog         | Max               |
| school      | Oxford University |
| river       | the Amazon River  |

Common nouns are usually not capitalized, unless they appear at the beginning of a sentence.

</LessonBlock> 
</ContentGrid>

## Capitalization

Common nouns are generally written with a lowercase letter.

<ContentGrid>
<LessonBlock type='example' title='Examples' fullWidth>

- I have a dog.
- She lives in a city.
- He is a teacher.
- We went to the park.

</LessonBlock>

<LessonBlock type='common_mistake' title='Do Not Capitalize Common Nouns' fullWidth>

- ❌ I have a Dog.
- ✅ I have a dog.

- ❌ She is a Teacher.
- ✅ She is a teacher.

</LessonBlock> 
</ContentGrid>
