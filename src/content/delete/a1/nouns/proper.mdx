A **proper noun** is the name of a specific **person**, **place**, **thing**, or **organization**. Proper nouns _always begin with a capital letter_.

<LessonBlock type='comparison' title='Common Nouns vs. Proper Nouns' >

| Common Noun | Proper Noun       |
| ----------- | ----------------- |
| country     | Chile             |
| city        | Rancagua          |
| person      | Maria             |
| month       | August            |
| day         | Monday            |
| school      | Oxford University |
| company     | Apple             |

</LessonBlock>

---

## Capitalization

Proper nouns always begin with a capital letter, even when they appear in the middle of a sentence.

<ContentGrid>
<LessonBlock type='rule' title='Use Capital Letters' fullWidth>

Use a capital letter for the first letter of a proper noun.

- John lives in Chile.
- I visited London last year.
- Monday is the first day of my English course.
- My birthday is in August.

</LessonBlock>

<LessonBlock type='common_mistake' title='Do Not Use Lowercase' fullWidth>

A proper noun should not normally begin with a lowercase letter.

- ❌ I live in chile.
- ✅ I live in Chile.
- ❌ We visited london.
- ✅ We visited London.

</LessonBlock>
</ContentGrid>

## Types of Proper Nouns

Proper nouns can refer to different types of specific names.

<ContentGrid>

<LessonBlock type='structure' title='People' fullWidth>

Names of specific people are proper nouns.

- James
- Sarah
- Michael Jackson
- Albert Einstein

</LessonBlock>

<LessonBlock type='structure' title='Places' fullWidth>

Names of specific countries, cities, continents, and geographical places are proper nouns.

- Chile
- Brazil
- Rancagua
- London
- South America
- the Pacific Ocean

</LessonBlock>

<LessonBlock type='structure' title='Days and Months' fullWidth>

Days of the week and months of the year are proper nouns.

- Monday
- Friday
- January
- August

In English, days and months are always capitalized.

</LessonBlock>

<LessonBlock type='structure' title='Organizations and Companies' fullWidth>

Names of specific organizations, schools, universities, and companies are proper nouns.

- Apple
- Microsoft
- Google
- Harvard University
- United Nations

</LessonBlock>

</ContentGrid>

---

## Proper Nouns in Sentences

Proper nouns can appear anywhere in a sentence, but their first letter remains capitalized.

<LessonBlock type='example' title='Examples' >

- Maria lives in Chile.
- Peter studies at Harvard University.
- We traveled to Brazil in January.
- Monday is my favorite day.
- Google is a technology company.

</LessonBlock>

<LessonBlock type='important' title='Remember' >

The main difference is:

- Common noun → a general name
- Proper noun → the specific name of a person, place, or thing

</LessonBlock>
