Quantifiers are words that tell us how much or how many of something there is.

They are commonly used before nouns to express an amount or quantity.

---

## Countable and Uncountable Nouns

The quantifier we use depends on whether the noun is countable or uncountable.

<div>

| Countable | Uncountable     | Both     |
| --------- | --------------- | -------- |
| many      | much            | some     |
| few       | little          | any      |
| a few     | a little        | a lot of |
| several   | a great deal of | lots of  |

</div>

### Countable nouns

Countable nouns are nouns that can be counted individually. They have both singular and plural forms.

Use many, few, a few, and **several** with plural countable nouns.

- many -> a large number
- few -> a small number, emphasizing that it is not enough
- a few -> a small number, but enough
- several -> more than two, but not many

#### Examples

- There are many students in the classroom.
- I have a few books to read.
- Few people understood the question.
- She has several friends in London.

---

### Uncountable nouns

Uncountable nouns are nouns that we cannot usually count as individual items. They generally do not have a plural form.

Use much, little, and a little with uncountable nouns.

- much → a large amount, usually in questions and negative sentences
- little → a small amount, emphasizing that it is not enough
- a little → a small amount, but enough for the situation

#### Examples:

- We don't have much time.
- There is little water left.
- I have a little money.

---

### Both

Use some, any, a lot of, and lots of with both types of nouns.

- some -> an unspecified amount or number
- any -> an unspecified amount or number, commonly in questions and negative sentences
- a lot of / lots of -> a large amount or number

#### Examples:

- I have some books. (countable)
- I have some money. (uncountable)
- Do you have any questions? (countable)
- We don't have any information. (uncountable)
- There are a lot of people here. (countable)
- There is a lot of water here. (uncountable)

## Quantifiers Table

<div>

| Quantifier         | Main Use                                    |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------------------- |
| many               | Questions and negative sentences            |
| much               | Questions and negative sentences            |
| some               | Affirmative sentences, offers, and requests |
| any                | Questions and negative sentences            |
| no / none          | Express zero quantity                       |
| a lot of / lots of | Informal alternative to _many_ and _much_   |
| few / a few        | Express a small number                      |
| little / a little  | Express a small amount                      |

</div>

## Common Mistakes

Much + Countable Noun

- ❌ I have much books.
- ✅ I have many books.

Many + Uncountable Noun

- ❌ There isn't many water.
- ✅ There isn't much water.

A Few + Uncountable Noun

- ❌ I need a few money.w
- ✅ I need a little money.

A Little + Countable Noun

- ❌ I have a little books.
- ✅ I have a few books.
